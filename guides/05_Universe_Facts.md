# 05 · Universe facts not in the rulebooks

*World-building the user set that applies across football universes, but isn't written in a rulebook in `structure/`. Campaign-specific lore belongs in that campaign's files, not here.*

---

## 1. Belgium

| Fact | |
|---|---|
| **KVC Osta Meerbeke** (real) | Three pitches: **A** for the first team; **B** (in the middle) for the reserves and U14–U19; **C** (left of the clubhouse) for U9–U13. Since 2024-25 the board has funding to make pitch A synthetic, finished around 2027-28 |
| **KVK Ninove** | See the Uplayer Rulebook: Uplayer co-coaching, all age groups in Interprovinciaal, training Monday, Wednesday and Friday |
| **The Voetbal Vlaanderen app** | In the VV Youth Rulebook v1.6, Chapter 12. Open to everyone. **Similar national youth-football apps exist in France, England, Spain and Germany** |

## 2. Europe (UEFA Youth Framework universes)

| Fact | |
|---|---|
| **Streaming** | UEFA youth competitions are streamed from the knockout stages onward |
| **UEFA Elite Summer** | A **pre-season tournament for U14–U19 in Los Angeles every year**. Entry: the **top three of every association's highest-level table** (e.g. Spain: the top three of Elite 1). **UEFA covers any non-professional team** |
| **Fouling** | No special rule against persistent fouling of one player: as in senior football |
