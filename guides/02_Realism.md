# 02 · Realism

*How results are produced. The short version: **rolls and models decide, never Claude's judgement**.*

---

## 1. A sim, not a storyline

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| **Results come from rolls** | Every match, scouting visit, approach, view count, selection and transfer is rolled with the models in `03_Models.md`, and logged in the file's simulation log |
| **No invented arcs** | No ups and downs written for drama. If the rolls say a boosted team wins every match, report that |
| **Nothing is guaranteed** | Projections, signings and outcomes are never treated as certain unless a rule makes them so (e.g. a perfect player's guarantee) |
| **Match moments** | An in-match decision moment for the user's player only exists when the match roll produces it |
| **Traits shape plans and odds** | A character's stated skill and traits shape both the decisions he makes and the odds rolled for him. Never roll an expert as an average person |
| **Don't underestimate the strong** | Elite clubs with elite players are rolled as elite. Professional academies' strength is weighted toward the top |
| **Don't overrate names** | A bigger or more famous club isn't automatically better; ratings come from the squad, the coach and the environment |
| **Injuries fit the body** | Never forced; odds follow the player's body and load |

## 2. Ratings

- **Universal FC26-style scale**: absolute and realistic, the same scale for a Gewestelijk U10 and a senior international. Not percentiles.
- **Potential is a hard ceiling.** A **sealed potential** (or any sealed trait) is read privately each season to shape development, and **revealed only when the user asks or the career ends**.
- Ratings appear in files as the narrator's tool only (*"Level (narrator)"*).

## 3. People

| Group | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Coaches** | Rated on **tactical intelligence** and **man management** (FC26-style). **Their licence (none, UEFA C, B, A, Pro) shapes those rolls.** Both ratings change results through the models |
| **Supporting cast** | **Continuous, not fixed**: teammates, former teammates, rivals and coaches are tracked every season (clubs, transfers, development, social lives) |
| **New players** | Every year new, younger cohorts arrive across the whole scene, in realistic numbers |
| **Looks** | Rolled at creation (1–10). Mostly physical, but also vibe/aura (grounded, intellectual, magnetic), wit, curiosity, competence, warmth, class, passion and style (digital footprint, lifestyle). **Looks and status matter most for social reach** |

## 4. Youth football rules that always apply

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| **Playing time** | Every player plays **at least half a match**. That's a minimum: a star or perfect player can play every full match |
| **No C teams** | Clubs field at most an A and a B team per age group |
| **Gewestelijk tables** | Mostly small clubs' **first** teams, not B-team leagues. **Only real, existing clubs.** Full tables of 14 by default |
| **Elite clubs** | **No B teams after U13** |
| **Pre-season** | Tournaments may be invented: **2–4 per pre-season**, mostly against Gewestelijk teams, sometimes Provinciaal. Elite teams usually play official tournaments |

## 5. Scouting and attention

- **Approaches are weighted by what each club actually saw**: matches against the player, the level he showed, the clean sheets or goals he produced against them. Never flat-chance rolls.
- Scouting includes **neighbouring regional clubs, professional academies and other clubs**, not only RBVA.

## 6. Social media

- **Reach is rolled**, never judged. Clips don't go viral most of the time.
- **Looks and status matter most**, especially for content where skill isn't needed (slideshows, photo dumps, "snap branchés").
- **Don't be too harsh either**: a good-looking teenager's snap branchés can average around 54k views (one known example hit 89k).
- **Fan editors exist** and already edit youth players (like La Masia's Hugo Galdeano and Destiny Kosiso).

## 7. The wider world

- **Roll senior football every season**: champions, promotions, relegations, the big tournaments. Apply its effects on youth football and on the protagonist.
- In UEFA-framework universes, the wider-world section analyses how the framework is working in big and small countries, plus the social pillar.
