# ROYAL BELGISCHE VOETBAL ACADEMIE
## Official Rulebook
### Consolidated edition — all rules in force

> This rulebook brings together every RBVA rule established from the academy's launch on 28 August 2026 to the latest amendments. Where a rule was changed, only the current version appears in the articles; earlier versions are listed in Annex A. Rules from outside RBVA that shape how it works (FIFA, federations, ministries) are listed separately in Part XI.

---

## PART I — IDENTITY AND GOVERNANCE

**Article 1 — Name.** The academy is the Royal Belgische Voetbal Academie (RBVA).

**Article 2 — Location.** RBVA is based in or near Brussels. All infrastructure, staff and facilities are complete and operational.

**Article 3 — Funding partners.** RBVA is sponsored by UEFA, the RBFA, the European Union, Voetbal Vlaanderen, ACFF and the governments of the Benelux countries.

**Article 4 — Voluntary participation.** No player is forced into any arrangement. Joining, the choice of schedule, residence and leaving are the player's and family's decisions, subject to the fee rules in Parts VIII and IX.

---

## PART II — AGE GROUPS AND TEAMS

**Article 5 — Age groups.** RBVA runs age groups from **U12 to U21**. After U21, players graduate and are released (see Part IX).

**Article 6 — Minimum teams.** Every age group has at least four teams: **A, B, C and D**.

**Article 7 — Sub-teams.** To prevent overcrowding, any tier may run additional sub-teams: A, A2, A3…; B, B2, B3…; and so on. A sub-team belongs to its tier for opponent level and transfer pricing (Article 42).

**Article 8 — Opponent levels.** Opponents are the youth teams of the following clubs:

| Tier | Plays against |
|---|---|
| **A** | Top-flight and first-division clubs; top private academies |
| **B** | B teams of those top-flight clubs; second-division clubs |
| **C** | B teams of second-division clubs; licensed third-tier clubs |
| **D** | Fourth-division clubs; B teams of third-tier clubs |

**Article 9 — International football.** Every team plays internationally. The main regions are **Europe, the United States and Saudi Arabia**.

**Article 10 — Match frequency.** Every team plays a **minimum of 5 and a maximum of 9** matches per month. Because of split schedules (Article 30), squads rotate naturally: in any given week some players are at RBVA and others at their clubs.

**Article 11 — Match format.** Home matches follow Belgian federation formats for the age group. Away matches follow the host's format.

**Article 12 — Streaming.** From **U13** onwards, every match is streamed live in full.

---

## PART III — COACHING AND TRAINING

**Article 13 — Licences.** Every RBVA team is coached by a holder of a **UEFA Pro or UEFA A** licence.

**Article 14 — Training frequency.**

| Age group | Sessions per week |
|---|---|
| U12–U15 | 2 |
| U16 and older | 3 |

**Article 15 — Rest days.** There is always at least one day without training between two sessions.

**Article 16 — Training hours.** No session may end later than **20:30**. Most sessions end before 20:00. Late sessions are reserved for emergency or catch-up training and for U18 and older.

---

## PART IV — SCOUTING

**Article 17 — Workforce.** RBVA employs **10,000 full-time domestic scouts** and **1,000 full-time international scouts**. Scouts work 56 hours a week, 8 hours a day, with rest periods.

**Article 18 — Allocation (domestic).**

| Youth tiers | Share of scouts |
|---|---|
| Gewestelijk and Provinciaal | 60% |
| Interprovincial, Elite 1 and Elite 2 | 40% |

**Article 19 — Roles.** Scouts are split into three roles, domestic and international alike:

| Role | Share | Working pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | 30% | Seasonal windows only (Article 20) |
| **Assessment** | 30% | Year-round, 8 hours a day |
| **Decision** | 40% | Year-round, 8 hours a day |

**Article 20 — Discovery windows.**

| Window | Dates |
|---|---|
| W1 | 28 August – 1 November |
| W2 | 30 December – 1 March |
| W3 | 1 April – 30 May |

**Article 21 — The three stages.**

1. **Discovery.** The scout performs an eye test and selects the player in the RBVA Database with one rating: *Low Priority, Moderate Priority, High Priority* or *Sign Immediately*. The scout then leaves.
2. **Assessment.** An assessor attends the player's following fixtures and gives a rating on the same four-point scale. The assessor then leaves.
3. **Decision.** A decider attends the next fixtures, knowing the discovery and assessment ratings, and decides: *Reject, Trial* or *Sign Immediately*.

There is no extended monitoring period.

**Article 22 — Resolution.** Every discovered case must be assessed, decided and fully resolved **within the same season**, and ideally within one month.

**Article 23 — The RBVA Database.** Every youth player is registered in the database. It can be filtered and sorted by geography, club, age, position (from U14) and tier.

**Article 24 — Notification.** Parents and the player's club are notified:
1. when the **assessment stage is complete**, without disclosure of any rating or outcome; and
2. when the **final decision** has been made.

**Article 25 — Scholarship.** A player rated *Sign Immediately* at both assessment and decision may receive a residential scholarship (Article 34).

---

## PART V — EXTERNAL SCOUTS AND VISITING CLUBS

**Article 26 — Open access.** Any scout, domestic or international, may watch RBVA training sessions and matches, with or without invitation, provided they wear a badge and prove they are a scout.

**Article 27 — VIP tickets.** Accredited scouts may request VIP tickets to any match, stating a clear and reasonable purpose.

**Article 28 — Trial invitations.** Scouts may send RBVA players trial invitations to their clubs.

**Article 29 — Interest disclosure.** Any club that shows clear interest in an RBVA player must give notice, with full transparency, to the player's coaches, who pass it on to the RBVA board. Families receive a regular count of registered interest. The consequences for voluntary departures are set out in Article 38.

---

## PART VI — CLUB ARRANGEMENTS

**Article 30 — Split schedules.** A player who signs with RBVA while at a club may choose:

| Option | Arrangement |
|---|---|
| 1 / 1 | 1 week RBVA, 1 week club |
| 2 / 1 | 2 consecutive weeks RBVA, 1 week club |
| 1 / 2 | 1 week RBVA, 2 consecutive weeks club |
| 2 / 2 | 2 weeks RBVA, 2 weeks club |
| Full-time | Leaves the club and plays only for RBVA |

**Article 31 — Lock period.** The chosen arrangement applies for at least **3 months**, after which the family may change it.

**Article 32 — Registered club.** The registered club is the club where the player is registered, or, if none, the club where the player was last registered. It receives the club share of every RBVA fee.

---

## PART VII — RESIDENCE, EDUCATION AND WELFARE

**Article 33 — Residential structure.** RBVA operates a residential structure for up to **30,000 people**, with top-tier nutrition and care.

**Article 34 — Residence fee.** Players who live far from Brussels may live permanently in the structure for a **one-time fee of 5% of their household's monthly income**. Scholarship players (Article 25) pay nothing.

**Article 35 — Study hall.** From the end of training until **22:00**, residents may use the study hall to study, receive educational help and catch up. Sandwiches, milk, water and healthy snacks are available.

**Article 36 — Expanded topsportstatuut.** Every player registered at RBVA automatically holds the **expanded topsportstatuut licence**, allowing them to leave school **at any time** as needed for football.

**36(2) — Scope (amended).** The expanded licence applies to **anyone and everyone registered at RBVA, without exception**: Flemish, French-speaking or German-speaking, and whatever school system they attend (Flemish Community, Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles, German-speaking Community, or international and private schools in Belgium). RBVA holds agreements with each education authority so that no RBVA player is limited by a regional absence allowance for RBVA activities.

---

## PART VIII — MOVEMENT WITHIN RBVA AND DEPARTURES

**Article 37 — Promotion, relegation and release.**
1. Players may be promoted or relegated between teams throughout the season.
2. There is **no promotion or relegation during the transfer windows** (January and August).
3. Promotion and relegation follow the published **Promotion Index**, a formula combining match data, training data and coach evaluation.
4. A player may be released after **three months of data**.

**Article 38 — Voluntary departure.** A player may leave RBVA voluntarily at any time. If a club has registered interest under Article 29 before the departure, and the player then joins a club covered by Article 40, the **tiered fee applies plus 50%**.

---

## PART IX — TRANSFER FEES

**Article 39 — Transfer windows.** RBVA transfers take place only in **January** and **August**.

**Article 40 — Scope.** Tiered fees apply, at every age, to:
1. all transfers to foreign clubs; and
2. domestic transfers to clubs in the **Pro League (1A)** and the **Challenger Pro League (1B)**, including a player's own registered club moving him into its first team.

**Article 41 — Tier prices.**

| Team | To registered club | To RBVA | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | €1,000,000 | €2,000,000 | **€3,000,000** |
| **B** | €200,000 | €300,000 | **€500,000** |
| **C** | €125,000 | €125,000 | **€250,000** |
| **D** | €50,000 | €75,000 | **€125,000** |

**Article 42 — Sub-team pricing.** For every sub-team level below the main team of a tier, the price falls by **5%** of the tier price, split in the same proportions.

| Team | Total | To registered club | To RBVA |
|---|---|---|---|
| A2 | €2,850,000 | €950,000 | €1,900,000 |
| A3 | €2,700,000 | €900,000 | €1,800,000 |
| B2 | €475,000 | €190,000 | €285,000 |
| C2 | €237,500 | €118,750 | €118,750 |
| D2 | €118,750 | €47,500 | €71,250 |

**Article 43 — Domestic transfers below 1B.** **No fee**, unless the receiving club is a **sister or affiliate club of a 1A or 1B club**. In that case the tiered fee applies (Article 41), as for a transfer to that 1A or 1B club.

**Article 44 — Tier Integrity Clause.** If a former RBVA player who left on or after 1 July 2027 is transferred again within **24 months** to a 1A or 1B club or abroad, the difference between the fee originally paid and the tier price applicable when he left RBVA is owed to RBVA and the registered club.

**Article 45 — Release before 21.** A player released by RBVA **before age 21** leaves with **no RBVA fee**.

**Article 46 — Graduation.** Players released at 21 on graduation who **have no professional contract** carry a graduate fee based on the **average team** they played in at RBVA:

| Average team | Graduate fee |
|---|---|
| C or D | **€5,000** |
| B or higher | **€1,000,000** |

---

## PART X — VISITING CLUBS

**Article 47 — Residential stays for visiting clubs.** Clubs travelling to play RBVA at its venue may stay in the residential structure with free food and shelter, for a fee:

| Travel mode | Base fee (up to 25 people) |
|---|---|
| Flying | **€4,000** |
| Bus | **€2,500** |

**Article 48 — Group size.** Everyone travelling counts: players, staff and other members of the delegation. For every person above 25, the fee rises by **5%** of the base fee.

| People | Flying | Bus |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | €4,000 | €2,500 |
| 30 | €5,000 | €3,125 |
| 35 | €6,000 | €3,750 |
| 45 | €8,000 | €5,000 |

## PART XI — EXTERNAL FRAMEWORK

These are not RBVA rules, but they are in force in the RBVA timeline and shape how the academy works.

| Rule | Body | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **International transfers** | FIFA (amended) | International transfers are legal at any age if the signing club fully covers or provides housing and travel. Players under 18 must bring at least one legal guardian of their choice. |
| **U11 tables and statistics** | Voetbal Vlaanderen (from 2028/29), RBFA for Wallonia (from 2030/31) | Official tables and statistics from U11 in Interprovincial, Elite 1 and Elite 2. |
| **Topsportstatuut licence** | Flemish and Walloon ministries of education (from 2032/33) | A licence, independent of the six official topsport sports, available at any age to Elite or top-division players. **Normal licence:** leave school up to 2 hours early. **RBVA expanded licence:** leave at any time. |

## ANNEX A — SUPERSEDED RULES

| Topic | Earlier rule | Replaced by |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer fees | Flat €100,000 to the club and €150,000 to RBVA for foreign transfers, even after 21; €50,000 each domestically | Tiered fees (Article 41) |

| Domestic loophole | €100,000 for all domestic transfers | Tiered fees for 1A and 1B (Article 40) |

| Interest register | Secret; framed publicly only as "so parents know how many are watching" | Fully public since the first case in February 2032 |
| Visiting clubs | €1,000 per flying club | Articles 47–48 |
| FIFA rule | International transfers from 13 | Any age (Part XI) |
| Match frequency | 2 to 4 matches per month | 5 to 9 matches per month (Article 10) |
| Domestic transfers below 1B | €50,000 to the registered club and €50,000 to RBVA | No fee, unless the club is a sister or affiliate of a 1A/1B club (Article 43) |
