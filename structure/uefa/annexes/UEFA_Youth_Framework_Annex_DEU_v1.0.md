# UEFA Youth Framework: Annex DEU

| | |
|---|---|
| **Version** | **1.0** |
| **Association** | Deutscher Fußball-Bund (DFB), with the **21 regional associations (Landesverbände)** |
| **Band** | **A** (Framework Art. 6.1): Elite 1 national (18), Elite 2 **Nord and Süd** (target 18 each), four levels below |
| **Scope** | All boys' youth league and cup football in Germany from **U8 to U19** |
| **In force** | **2029-30** (seeded from 2028-29, Framework Art. 8.2) |
| **Replaces** | The **DFB-Nachwuchsliga** (U17 and U19), the **Junioren-Regionalligen** and the regional associations' top youth leagues above the Local level |
| **Unchanged** | Registration, discipline, safeguarding (DFB and Landesverband regulations). International transfers of minors: **Framework Art. 8.4** inside UEFA, FIFA RSTP Art. 19 outside it |

---

## Chapter 1: Definitions

### Article 1.1: Age groups
1. **One birth year per age group**, U8 to U19. **U19 is the last.**
2. The traditional names stay as **labels** for pairs of age groups:

| Label | Age groups |
|---|---|
| F-Junioren | U8, U9 |
| E-Junioren | U10, U11 |
| D-Junioren | U12, U13 |
| C-Junioren | U14, U15 |
| B-Junioren | U16, U17 |
| A-Junioren | U18, U19 |

3. Younger players may always play up.

### Article 1.2: Formats and match length

| Age groups | Format | Match length |
|---|---|---|
| U8–U11 | **7v7** | 2 × 25 |
| **U12–U13** | **9v9** | 2 × 30 |
| U14–U15 | 11v11 | 2 × 35 |
| U16 | 11v11 | 2 × 40 |
| U17–U19 | 11v11 | 2 × 45 |

### Article 1.3: Standings
**From U10.** Win 3, draw 1, loss 0. Tiebreakers: goal difference, goals scored, head-to-head.

### Article 1.4: Professional clubs
A club whose first team plays, on the first day of the season, in the **Bundesliga, 2. Bundesliga or 3. Liga** (Framework Art. 6.1). **A club's second team in the 3. Liga or Regionalliga does not make it professional.**

---

## Chapter 2: The levels

### Article 2.1: Six levels from U12

| Rank | Level | German name | Tables per age group | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Elite 1** | **Junioren-Bundesliga** | **1 national** | **18** |
| 2 | **Elite 2** | **Junioren-Bundesliga Nord / Süd** | **2** | **Target 18 each** |
| 3 | Interregional | **Junioren-Regionalliga** | **5**: Nord, Nordost, West, Südwest, Süd | 16 |
| 4 | Regional | **Oberliga** | Per Landesverband | Up to 16 |
| 5 | District | **Landesliga / Bezirksliga** | Per Landesverband, by district | Up to 16 |
| 6 | Local | **Kreisliga** | By Kreis | Up to 14; parallel tables when needed |

**U10–U11**: Kreis level only, standings kept. **U8–U9**: no standings.

### Article 2.2: Elite 2 geography
| Group | Landesverbände |
|---|---|
| **Nord** | Schleswig-Holstein, Hamburg, Bremen, Niedersachsen, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Berlin, Brandenburg, Sachsen-Anhalt, **Westfalen, Niederrhein** |
| **Süd** | Mittelrhein, Rheinland, Südwest, Saarland, Hessen, Thüringen, Sachsen, Bayern, Baden, Südbaden, Württemberg |

### Article 2.3: Formats
Every table is a **double round robin**: Elite 1, **34 matches**; Elite 2, 34; Interregional, 30.

## Chapter 3: Promotion and relegation

| Between | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Elite 1 ↔ Elite 2** | **Bottom two down**, by geography; **each Elite 2 group winner up** (Framework Art. 3.4.1). Passing down: Annex BEL 4.8.3 |
| **Elite 2 ↔ Interregional** | **Each of the five Regionalliga winners up**, if licensed; Elite 2 relegates as many, by geography; none while below target |
| **Below** | Each Landesverband runs its own, with **at least two up and two down** between every pair of levels |
| **U19** | Promotion or relegation earned at U19 **lapses** |

---

## Chapter 4: Clubs and teams

1. **Professional clubs**: one team per age group (Framework Art. 4.2).
2. **Second teams of other clubs**: start two levels below the first team, never into Elite (Framework Art. 4.3).
3. **U19 players and the second team**: a U19 player may play for his club's **second team** (3. Liga or Regionalliga) and its U19 in the same season, but **not in the same weekend**.

---

## Chapter 5: Travel, match days, season

1. **Travel**: the DFB pays trips of **150 km or more** for **non-professional clubs** at Elite and Interregional level; professional clubs pay their own (Framework Art. 5.2).
2. **Match days**: **Saturday or Sunday**, kick-off 10:00–18:00; continental weeks under Framework Art. 5.4.
3. **Season**: August to May; **winter break** from the Sunday before Christmas to the second weekend of January.

---

## Chapter 6: DFB-Pokal der Junioren

1. **U15 to U19**, one cup per age group; **only first teams**.
2. Entry by the cohort's previous season: **Round 1**: the five Regionalliga winners and the Landesverband cup winners · **Round 2**: all Elite 2 teams · **Round 3 (last 32)**: all Elite 1 teams.
3. Single matches, lower level at home; **semi-finals and final single matches**, the final at a neutral venue.
4. Draws: **extra time, then penalties** (U15: straight to penalties).
5. **The winner takes Germany's cup place** in the continental competitions (Framework Art. 11.3).

---

## Chapter 7: Elite licence
1. **The DFL** audits professional clubs (it already certifies Nachwuchsleistungszentren); **the DFB** audits the rest, under Framework Art. 3.5.
2. **Elite 1 home matches** on a full-size pitch; a coach with the **UEFA A licence** for every Elite 1 team.
3. A club that houses players under 18 must meet **the youth welfare rules of its Land** and Framework Art. 8.4.

---

## Chapter 8: Seeding (one-off, 2029-30)
Elite 1 per age group: **the 18 clubs whose cohorts played the real top level in 2028-29** (the DFB-Nachwuchsliga's Liga A for U17 and U19, the strongest Regionalliga teams below), by the DFB's youth ranking, **at least seven in each Elite 2 half's territory**. Elite 2: the next 36, by geography.
