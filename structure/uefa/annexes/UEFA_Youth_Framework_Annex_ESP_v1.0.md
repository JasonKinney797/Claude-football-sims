# UEFA Youth Framework: Annex ESP

| | |
|---|---|
| **Version** | **1.0** |
| **Association** | Real Federación Española de Fútbol (RFEF), with the **19 territorial federations** |
| **Band** | **A** (Framework Art. 6.1): Elite 1 national, Elite 2 **North and South**, four levels below |
| **Scope** | All boys' youth league and cup football in Spain from **U8 to U19** |
| **In force** | **2029-30** (seeded from 2028-29, Framework Art. 8.2) |
| **Replaces** | The two-year categories (Alevín, Infantil, Cadete, Juvenil), the **División de Honor Juvenil** (7 groups of 16), the **Liga Nacional Juvenil**, the **Copa de Campeones**, and the territorial youth leagues above the Local level |
| **Unchanged** | Registration, discipline and safeguarding (RFEF regulations). International transfers of minors: **Framework Art. 8.4** inside UEFA, FIFA RSTP Art. 19 outside it |

---

## Chapter 1: Definitions

### Article 1.1: Age groups
1. Every age group is **one birth year**, from **U8 to U19** (Framework Art. 2.1). **U19 is the last age group.**
2. The traditional names stay as **labels for pairs of age groups** in everyday use, with no competition meaning:

| Label | Age groups |
|---|---|
| Benjamín | U9, U10 |
| Alevín | U11, U12 |
| Infantil | U13, U14 |
| Cadete | U15, U16 |
| Juvenil | U17, U18, U19 |

3. **Younger players may always play up** (Framework Art. 2.1). A player plays for **one team of his club per weekend**.

### Article 1.2: Team and cohort
Each season a team **moves up one age group** with its players. **The level belongs to the cohort, not to the age group** (Framework Art. 2.2).

### Article 1.3: Formats and match length

| Age groups | Format | Match length |
|---|---|---|
| U8–U12 | **Fútbol 7** (7v7) | U8–U10: 2 × 25 · U11–U12: 2 × 30 |
| **U13** | **11v11** | 2 × 35 |
| U14–U15 | 11v11 | 2 × 35 (Framework Art. 2.5) |
| U16 | 11v11 | 2 × 40 |
| U17–U19 | 11v11 | 2 × 45 |

**Why 11v11 at U13:** Spain has played Infantil football 11-a-side for decades; the Framework lets the annex choose below U14 (Art. 2.5.2).

### Article 1.4: Standings
**From U10.** Win 3, draw 1, loss 0. Tiebreakers in order: **head-to-head points, head-to-head goal difference** (the RFEF's traditional order), then overall goal difference, goals scored, fair play. Points per match whenever tables of different sizes are compared.

### Article 1.5: Professional clubs
A **professional club** is one whose senior first team plays, on the first day of the season, in **LaLiga EA Sports or LaLiga Hypermotion** (Framework Art. 4.1). **Filial (reserve) teams in Primera or Segunda Federación don't make a club professional**; the club's status follows its first team.

## Chapter 2: The levels

### Article 2.1: Six levels from U13

| Rank | Level | Spanish name | Tables per age group | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Elite 1** | **División de Honor** | **1 national** | **20** |
| 2 | **Elite 2** | **Liga Nacional** | **2: Norte and Sur** | **Target 16 each** |
| 3 | Interregional | **Liga Interterritorial** | **6 groups** (Art. 2.2) | 16 |
| 4 | Regional | **Preferente** | Per territorial federation | Up to 18 |
| 5 | District | **Primera Territorial** | Per federation, by district | Up to 16 |
| 6 | Local | **Segunda Territorial** | Per federation, by district | Up to 16; **splits into parallel tables when one isn't enough** |

**Below U13** (U10–U12, Fútbol 7) there are three levels, all run by the territorial federations: **Preferente, Primera, Segunda**. U8–U9: no standings.

### Article 2.2: Geography

| Elite 2 | Federations |
|---|---|
| **Liga Nacional Norte** | Galicia, Asturias, Cantabria, País Vasco, Navarra, La Rioja, Castilla y León, Aragón, **Catalunya** |
| **Liga Nacional Sur** | Madrid, Castilla-La Mancha, Extremadura, Comunitat Valenciana, Región de Murcia, Andalucía, Islas Baleares, Islas Canarias, Ceuta, Melilla |

**Interregional, 6 groups:** I Galicia–Asturias–Cantabria–Castilla y León · II País Vasco–Navarra–La Rioja–Aragón · III Catalunya–Islas Baleares · IV Madrid–Castilla-La Mancha–Extremadura · V Comunitat Valenciana–Murcia · VI Andalucía–Canarias–Ceuta–Melilla.

**Canarias, Baleares, Ceuta and Melilla**: their Elite and Interregional teams play in the mainland groups; **travel is paid** (Art. 5.1).

### Article 2.3: Formats
Every table is a **double round robin**: Elite 1, **38 matches**; Elite 2, 30; Interregional, 30.

## Chapter 3: Promotion and relegation

### Article 3.1: Elite 1 ↔ Elite 2
1. **The bottom two of Elite 1 go down** (one to each Elite 2 group, by geography).
2. **Each Elite 2 group winner goes up** (Framework Art. 3.4.1).
3. Passing down, declining and vacancies follow **Annex BEL Articles 4.8.3, 4.10 and 4.11** (Framework Art. 3.4.4): the place passes to the next eligible team in the same group, down to 4th.

### Article 3.2: Elite 2 ↔ Interregional
1. **Each Interregional group winner goes up**, if licensed: **six promotions**, three to each Elite 2 group by geography.
2. **Elite 2 relegates as many as come up**, bottom first, split by geography.
3. **Below target** (fewer than 16 in a group): no relegation from that group (Framework Art. 3.4.3).

### Article 3.3: Below Interregional
Each territorial federation runs promotion and relegation between Preferente, Primera and Segunda Territorial, and promotes **its Preferente champion** to the Interregional group (play-offs where a federation has several Preferente tables). **At least two up and two down** between every pair of levels.

### Article 3.4: U19
Promotion or relegation earned in **U19 lapses** (the cohort leaves youth football).

## Chapter 4: Clubs and teams

### Article 4.1: Professional clubs
**One team per age group** (Framework Art. 4.2). **Barcelona, Real Madrid and every other LaLiga club field no B or C youth teams**; the traditional "Juvenil A / Juvenil B" are now **the U19 and U18 teams**.

### Article 4.2: Second teams of other clubs
1. Start **two levels below** the first team, never below Segunda Territorial (Framework Art. 4.3).
2. May climb **up to one level below the first team**, and **never into Elite**.
3. **No second team beside an Elite team**.

### Article 4.3: Players with the filial and the first team
1. A youth player registered with his club's **filial** (Primera or Segunda Federación) or **first team** may still play for his age group's team **in the same weekend only if he didn't play senior minutes that weekend**.
2. A player who plays **senior minutes in five or more matches** in a season may no longer play **Elite youth league matches** that season; he may still play the **Copa del Rey** of his age group and the continental competitions.

### Article 4.4: No feeder Elite teams
Framework Art. 4.4 applies: no in-season loans into or out of an Elite team.

---

## Chapter 5: Travel, calendar, match days

### Article 5.1: Travel
1. **The RFEF pays** every trip of **150 km or more** for **non-professional clubs** at Elite and Interregional level, and **every inter-island and island-mainland trip** for clubs from Canarias, Baleares, Ceuta and Melilla.
2. **Professional clubs pay their own way** from U13 (Framework Art. 5.2).

### Article 5.2: Match days
1. **Elite and Interregional: Saturday or Sunday**, kick-off between **10:00 and 19:00**; in **July–September and May–June**, not between 13:00 and 17:00 in Andalucía, Murcia, Extremadura, Comunitat Valenciana and Castilla-La Mancha (heat).
2. Federation levels: the territorial federation sets the day.
3. Continental match days: Framework Art. 5.4.

### Article 5.3: Season
**September to May.** The last league matchday is **no later than the second Sunday of May**; the Copa del Rey final is the last weekend of May or the first of June, never clashing with a continental final.

## Chapter 6: Copa del Rey (youth)

### Article 6.1: Age groups
**U14 to U19**, every age group its own cup. **Only first teams.**

### Article 6.2: Entry (by the cohort's previous season)

| Round | Who enters |
|---|---|
| Round 1 | **Every Interregional table winner and runner-up**, and every Preferente champion |
| Round 2 | **All Elite 2 teams** |
| Round 3 (last 32) | **All Elite 1 teams**; straight knockout from here |

1. **Single matches**, the lower-level team at home; equal level, by draw. From the semi-finals: **two legs**. **Final: one match, neutral venue.**
2. **Draws: extra time, then penalties** (U14–U15: straight to penalties, Framework Art. 16).
3. **The winner takes Spain's cup place** in the continental competitions (Framework Art. 11.3).

---

## Chapter 7: Elite licence

### Article 7.1: Who audits
**LaLiga** audits professional clubs; **the RFEF** audits everyone else, both under Framework Art. 3.5 (Annex BEL Chapter 5 criteria, two tiers).

### Article 7.2: Spanish additions
1. **Full-size pitch** (natural grass or approved synthetic) for every Elite home match.
2. **A coach with the UEFA A licence** for every Elite 1 team; UEFA B for Elite 2.
3. **Residencia**: a club that houses players under 18 must meet the RFEF's residence standards and the **regional child-protection rules** of its autonomous community, and guarantee schooling (ESO, Bachillerato or FP) under Framework Art. 8.4.

---

## Chapter 8: Seeding (one-off, 2029-30)

### Article 8.1: Elite 1
Per age group, **the 20 clubs whose cohorts played the real top youth competitions in 2028-29** (División de Honor Juvenil for U17–U19; the strongest territorial Cadete and Infantil leagues below), ranked by the RFEF's 2028-29 youth ranking, with **at least eight Elite 1 places in each Elite 2 half's territory**.

### Article 8.2: Elite 2 and below
Elite 2: the next 32 by the same ranking, split by geography. Interregional: the rest of the 2028-29 Liga Nacional Juvenil and the territorial top divisions, by ranking.
