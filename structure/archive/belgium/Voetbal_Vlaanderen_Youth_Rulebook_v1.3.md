# Rulebook: Voetbal Vlaanderen Youth Competition Structure

| | |
|---|---|
| **Version** | **1.3 (consolidated)**: v1.0 plus amendments A1–A26, built into the articles |
| **Adopted** | **August 2025** |
| **Structuring season** | **2025-26** (played under the old rules) |
| **First competitive season** | **2026-27** |
| **Accepted by** | Voetbal Vlaanderen, KBVB/RBFA, Pro League, ACFF (the francophone wing, renamed **FFA** in April 2026) |

---

## Chapter 1: Scope and definitions

### Article 1.1: Scope
This rulebook governs all **boys' youth competitions of Voetbal Vlaanderen from U6 up to and including U19**, the national Elite youth competitions and the Youth Beker van België. Girls who play in boys' teams do so under the existing Voetbal Vlaanderen dispensation rules.

### Article 1.2: Age groups
Every age group is **one birth year** (U6, U7, U8 ... U19). **U19 is the last youth age group.**

### Article 1.3: Cohort
A cohort is a club's team in one age group. Each season the cohort **moves up one age group** (the U12 of this season is the U13 of next season). **The competitive level belongs to the cohort, not to the age group.**

### Article 1.4: Levels

| Rank | Level | Short | Age groups |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Elite 1 | E1 | **U13 to U19** |
| 2 | Elite 2 | E2 | **U13 to U19** |
| 3 | Interprovinciaal | IP | U10 to U19 |
| 4 | Provinciaal | P | U10 to U19 |
| 5 | Gewestelijk | G | U6 to U19 |

1. Within one level there can be **parallel tables** (table A, table B, and so on). Tables at the same level are equal in strength; the letter carries no rank.
2. **U6 to U9 play Gewestelijk.** Exception: where a club's U10 plays Provinciaal or Interprovinciaal, that club's U6 to U9 may play at that level too.
3. **Elite clubs below Elite age:** a club holding an Elite licence plays its **U10 to U12 in Interprovinciaal** and its **U6 to U9 in Gewestelijk**. These U10–U12 teams **take IP places by right**: they are placed in IP before any ranking is applied, inside the two tables of 14.

### Article 1.5: Match formats

| Format | Age groups |
|---|---|
| 2v2 | **Abolished** |
| 5v5 | U6 to U8 |
| 8v8 | U9 to U13 |
| 11v11 | U14 to U19 |

### Article 1.6: Standings
Standings are kept **from U10 onwards**, at every level from Gewestelijk up. U6 to U9 play without standings.

### Article 1.7: Registered home ground
Every club registers **one home ground per season**. All distances in this rulebook are measured from it. A club can change its registered home ground **only between seasons**.

### Article 1.8: Distance and "closer"
1. All distances are **crow-flies** from the registered home ground, unless an article says otherwise.
2. **"Closer"** (Articles 2.3 and 2.4) means the **average crow-flies distance** from the club's registered home ground to the other teams of the table it would join.

## Chapter 2: Geography

### Article 2.1: Arrondissements

| Province | Arrondissements |
|---|---|
| Antwerpen | Antwerpen, Mechelen, Turnhout |
| Limburg | Hasselt, Maaseik, Tongeren |
| Oost-Vlaanderen | Aalst, Dendermonde, Eeklo, Gent, Oudenaarde, Sint-Niklaas |
| Vlaams-Brabant | Halle-Vilvoorde, Leuven, plus Brussel (Article 2.2) |
| West-Vlaanderen | Brugge, Diksmuide, Ieper, Kortrijk, Oostende, Roeselare, Tielt, Veurne |

### Article 2.2: Brussels
For Voetbal Vlaanderen clubs, the **Brussels-Capital Region counts as an arrondissement of Vlaams-Brabant**.

### Article 2.3: Gewestelijk scope
1. A Gewestelijk table only contains teams from **the same arrondissement**, or from **municipalities directly bordering that arrondissement** in another arrondissement.
2. A club in a border municipality belongs to its own arrondissement, unless the federation places it in the neighbouring arrondissement's Gewestelijk because that is closer (Article 1.8).
3. That placement is **fixed for the whole season**, and the club then belongs to that arrondissement for the Gewestelijk play-offs and promotion.

### Article 2.4: Provinciaal scope
Provinciaal covers **the whole province plus exactly two bordering arrondissements of another province**. Each border arrondissement is claimed by one province only:

| Province | Claims |
|---|---|
| West-Vlaanderen | Eeklo, Oudenaarde |
| Oost-Vlaanderen | Tielt, Halle-Vilvoorde |
| Antwerpen | Sint-Niklaas, Dendermonde |
| Vlaams-Brabant | Mechelen, Hasselt |
| Limburg | Turnhout, Leuven |

1. A club in a claimed arrondissement plays in its own province's Provinciaal **by default**.
2. The federation can place it in the claiming province's Provinciaal when that is closer (Article 1.8).
3. A placed club **counts entirely for the province it plays in**: tables, play-offs, quotas, promotion and relegation.
4. The placement is fixed per season.

### Article 2.5: Interprovinciaal scope
Interprovinciaal covers **all five Flemish provinces plus the Voetbal Vlaanderen clubs of Brussels**. IP tables are drawn **fully mixed**, without geographic grouping.

### Article 2.6: Elite scope
Elite 1 and Elite 2 are **national competitions under the KBVB** and include clubs of both wings.

## Chapter 3: Season and calendar

### Article 3.1: One series per season
Every level plays **one full-season series**. There are no autumn or spring series.

### Article 3.2: Saturday
League matches are played on **Saturdays**, one match per team per Saturday.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kick-off window** | 09:00 to 18:00 |
| **Matches with travel of 50 km or more** (Article 6.1) | Not before 11:00 |

**Exception for U6 to U9** (no standings): matches may also be played on **Sunday mornings**.

### Article 3.3: Wednesday afternoon
Wednesday afternoons are reserved for:
1. the **six fixed cup Wednesdays** of the Youth Beker van België;
2. **catch-up and emergency** league matches, on the other Wednesdays.

### Article 3.4: Postponements and forfeits
1. A postponed league match is replayed on a Wednesday afternoon **within three weeks**.
2. If it is not played by then, **the team that caused the postponement loses by forfeit**.
3. **No forfeit for force majeure:** weather, an absent referee (Article 10.8), an unplayable pitch through no fault of the club, or a failure of federation transport (Article 6.6). The match is replayed within three weeks.
4. A forfeit is only given **for the club's own fault**.

### Article 3.5: Pairing of age groups
Clubs and fixture planners pair consecutive age groups so that, where possible, **one is at home when the other plays away** (for example U10 at home while U11 plays away).

### Article 3.6: Table sizes

| Level | Size | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Elite 1 | **16 teams** per age group | Double round robin |
| Elite 2 | All remaining licensed clubs | **Double round robin with ten or more teams; quadruple round robin with fewer than ten** |
| Interprovinciaal | **Two tables of 14** per age group | Double round robin |
| Provinciaal | **Tables of 14**; number of tables per province fixed each season by the federation (Article 4.6.5) | Double round robin |
| Gewestelijk | **At most 14 teams per table**; splits into parallel tables when one table is not enough | Double round robin |

### Article 3.7: Points and ranking
**Win 3, draw 1, loss 0.** Ranking order:
1. points
2. matches won
3. goal difference
4. goals scored
5. away goals scored
6. away matches won

When tables of different sizes must be compared, **points per match** is used first.

### Article 3.8: Play-off window and cup end dates
1. **Play-off window:** the **three Saturdays after the last league matchday**, for the arrondissement, provincial and relegation play-offs.
2. **Cup semi-finals:** the Saturday after the play-off window. **Cup final:** one Saturday later (Article 7.3).

## Chapter 4: Promotion and relegation

### Article 4.1: Cohort principle
A level earned at the end of a season in age group N applies to **the same cohort in age group N+1** the following season.

> **Example:** a U10 team that wins promotion to Provinciaal plays Provinciaal as U11.

### Article 4.2: Entry rule for U10
A club's new U10 **starts at the level the club's U11 plays that same season**. That is the level the previous U10 cohort earned.

### Article 4.3: Last age group
Promotion or relegation earned in **U19 lapses**. Promotion or relegation earned in U13 carries into 11v11 at U14 as normal.

### Article 4.4: Gewestelijk to Provinciaal
1. Per arrondissement and per age group, **the top two of every Gewestelijk table** go to the arrondissement play-offs, within the play-off window (Article 3.8).
2. The play-offs are a **single knockout**. When the number of teams is not a power of two, table winners get byes, best points per match first.
3. The better-ranked team plays at home; the final is played at a **neutral venue** named by the provincial committee.
4. **The winner is promoted** to Provinciaal.

### Article 4.5: Provinciaal to Interprovinciaal
1. Per province and per age group, **the top two of every Provinciaal table** go to the provincial play-offs, within the play-off window.
2. Single knockout, with byes for the best table winners when needed. **The two semi-final winners are promoted** to Interprovinciaal. The final decides the provincial title only.
3. Result: **two promotions per province, ten per age group** across Flanders.

### Article 4.6: Provinciaal relegation
1. Per province, the number of relegated teams equals **the number needed to bring every Provinciaal table back to 14** next season, **with a minimum of two**.
2. **Relegation play-off:** the 13th and 14th of every table are cross-paired against teams from other tables (13th of one table against 14th of another). The better-ranked team plays at home. **Losers are relegated.**
3. If more relegations are needed than there are losers, the lowest-ranked remaining teams are relegated by points per match, starting with the 12th places.
4. If fewer relegations are needed than there are losers, the losers with the best points per match are reprieved, never going below the minimum of two.
5. **The federation fixes next season's number of Provinciaal tables per province by 1 March** at the latest.

### Article 4.7: Interprovinciaal relegation
1. **The bottom five of each IP table are relegated**: ten per age group.
2. If more relegations are needed to keep IP at 28 teams, the next lowest teams by points per match are relegated as well.
3. A relegated team goes to the Provinciaal of the province it belongs to, including a placement made under Article 2.4.

### Article 4.8: No promotion from Interprovinciaal to Elite
Elite places are reached **only through the licence** in Chapter 5, never on the pitch.

### Article 4.9: Elite 1 and Elite 2
1. Per age group, **the bottom two of Elite 1 drop to Elite 2** and **the top two of Elite 2 move up to Elite 1**, under the cohort principle. Both clubs must hold an Elite licence.
2. **Elite 1 vacancy:** filled by the **best-placed Elite 2 team that didn't move up**.

### Article 4.10: Accepting or declining promotion
1. A club accepts promotion **in writing within seven days after the play-off final**. **No answer counts as a denial.**
2. A declined spot passes:
   - **into Provinciaal:** to the play-off runner-up, then to the other play-off participants in order of points per match;
   - **into Interprovinciaal:** to **the losing semi-finalists, by points per match**, then to the other play-off participants in order of points per match.
3. If nobody takes it, the spot stays empty and that level relegates one team fewer.

### Article 4.11: Vacancies
A club that cannot field a cohort in the next season **loses its spot**. The spot goes to the next team in line: first the best-placed team that missed promotion in the play-offs of the level below, then a reprieved relegated team.

### Article 4.12: Second and further teams (clubs without an Elite licence)
1. **Starting level:** a second team starts **two levels below its club's first team in the same age group, but never below Gewestelijk**.

| First team | Second team starts in |
|---|---|
| Interprovinciaal | Gewestelijk |
| Provinciaal | Gewestelijk |
| Gewestelijk | Gewestelijk, in a different table |

2. **Third and further teams** start in Gewestelijk. A club may enter **as many Gewestelijk teams as it wants**.
3. **Never in the same table** as another team of the same club, at any level.
4. A second team **can be promoted up to its first team's level**, and then plays in a **separate table** at that level.
5. **The team at the higher level is always the first team.** If a second team ends a season above its first team, the designations **swap at the end of the season**.
6. Second and further teams **do not play in the Youth Beker van België**.

### Article 4.13: Squad protection
1. During **the last five matchdays** of the season and during the play-offs, no player can move **between any two teams of the same club** in the same age group, **in either direction**.
2. From U6 up to and including U13, **every player on the match sheet plays at least half of the match**.
3. The existing Voetbal Vlaanderen **dispensation rules** (late developers, girls) remain in force unchanged.

### Article 4.14: Mergers

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Any merger | The merged club takes **the higher of the two levels** in every age group |
| The two clubs played at different levels | The second team **keeps the lower level** |
| The two clubs played at the same level | The second team **starts one level lower** |

A merged club holding an Elite licence keeps **one team per age group only** (Article 5.9).

## Chapter 5: Elite licence

### Licence criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Sporting** (Art. 5.1) | Senior first team in the JPL or CPL for **three consecutive completed seasons**, counting the season of the request. A club's U23 team in the CPL does not count as its senior team |
| **Pitches** (Art. 5.2) | At least **two full-size synthetic pitches and one full-size natural grass pitch**, owned or leased for at least a full season, used exclusively by the club |
| **Medical** (Art. 5.3) | At least **two full-time medical staff**, at least one a physiotherapist, with a sports doctor reachable on every match day |
| **Buses** (Art. 5.4) | **One team bus per age group** with an Elite team, owned or leased for at least a full season, used exclusively by that age group |

### Article 5.1: Sporting condition
1. See the table above. **The request season counts** toward the three seasons if the club is still professional at the end of it.
2. **Renting pitches from another club for the audit does not count** toward Article 5.2, and **a bus cannot be shared between two age groups** under Article 5.4.

### Article 5.5: Audit and appeal
The licence is checked **every season** through the existing academy licence audit. Decisions are published **by 30 April**. Appeals go to the **Belgian Court of Arbitration for Sport (BAS)**.

### Article 5.6: Loss of professional status
1. When the senior first team is no longer professional, the club's youth teams **leave Elite from the following season**.
2. Every age group of that club **enters Interprovinciaal as an extra team, in the IP table with the fewest teams**. IP relegates the same number of extra teams that season, so the table sizes are back on target a year later.
3. From that season the club may field second teams again under Article 4.12.
4. In the Youth Beker van België, these age groups **enter at the IP stage**.
5. To return to Elite, the club must meet the full criteria again. **The three-season count restarts from zero.**
6. Clubs of the francophone wing enter the equivalent FFA level instead of Voetbal Vlaanderen IP.

### Article 5.7: Loss of licence for other reasons
A club that keeps professional status but **fails the infrastructure, medical or bus criteria** is treated exactly as in Article 5.6, points 1 to 4. It may return once it passes the audit again; **the three-season count is not reset**.

### Article 5.8: Entering Elite
1. When a club gets the licence, **all its Elite age groups (U13 to U19) go to Elite 2** the next season.
2. The places they leave go to the next team in line under Article 4.11.
3. **The club's second and further teams are dissolved from its first Elite season.** Their players are free to change club in the normal transfer window.

### Article 5.9: One team per age group
A club holding an Elite licence fields **exactly one team per age group, from U6 to U19**. It has **no second or further teams** at any level.

### Article 5.10: Travel
Elite clubs are **excluded from federation travel coverage** (Chapter 6), since the licence requires them to have their own buses.

## Chapter 6: Federation travel

### Article 6.1: Principle
Every match that requires travel of **50 km or more, measured as the crow flies** from the travelling team's registered home ground to the venue, is **fully paid by the RBFA**.

| Applies to | Does not apply to |
|---|---|
| Gewestelijk, Provinciaal, Interprovinciaal | Elite |
| League matches, play-offs, cup matches | Trips under 50 km |
| Both teams at a neutral venue | |

### Article 6.2: Who pays and who organises
1. **The RBFA pays.**
2. **Voetbal Vlaanderen** (and the **FFA** for its clubs) **organises and contracts** the transport: bus, train or any other means.

### Article 6.3: Door to door
1. Transport **picks the team up at its registered home ground** at a scheduled time.
2. It brings the team to a drop-off point **no more than 10 minutes' walk (about 800 m) from the dressing rooms** at the venue. If a bus cannot get that close, **the federation arranges a shuttle**.
3. **The return trip is included**: pickup at the same point, **no later than one hour after the final whistle**, back to the registered home ground.
4. The coach receives **the full travel details one week before the match**.

### Article 6.4: Who travels
**The players on the match sheet and up to four staff members. Parents are not covered.**

### Article 6.5: Own transport
A club that chooses to travel by its own means **receives no reimbursement**.

### Article 6.6: Transport failure
If federation transport fails or arrives late, **no team loses by forfeit**. The match is replayed on a Wednesday within three weeks.

## Chapter 7: Youth Beker van België

### Article 7.1: Age groups and wings
**U13 to U19**, played jointly by Voetbal Vlaanderen and FFA clubs. U13 plays 8v8. **Only first teams take part** (Article 4.12.6).

### Article 7.2: Qualification
Qualification is based on **the cohort's previous season** (for U13, the U12 season).

| Round | Who enters |
|---|---|
| Round 1 | **Provinciaal table winners**, plus the FFA's provincial-level qualifiers |
| Round 2 | Interprovinciaal teams that finished **in the top half** of their table, plus the FFA's interprovincial-level qualifiers |
| Round 3 | **All Elite 2 clubs** |
| Round 4 | **All Elite 1 clubs**; straight knockout to the final from here |

1. The FFA quotas are set every season so that **exactly 32 teams play round 4**.
2. **Byes only in rounds 1 to 3.** When an even number is impossible in those rounds, the remaining bye goes by draw to a team entering in that round.

### Article 7.3: Dates

| Stage | When |
|---|---|
| Rounds 1 to 6 | The six fixed cup Wednesdays |
| Semi-finals | The Saturday after the play-off window (Article 3.8) |
| Final | One Saturday later, at **Proximus Basecamp, Tubize** |

### Article 7.4: Home team
**The lower-level team plays at home.** When both teams play at the same level, the first team drawn plays at home.

### Article 7.5: Drawn matches

| Age groups | Rule |
|---|---|
| U13 to U15 | Straight to penalties |
| U16 to U19 | Two halves of extra time, then penalties |

### Article 7.6: Travel
Chapter 6 applies to **all cup matches**, including the semi-finals and the final.

## Chapter 8: Play-off draws in the league structure

### Article 8.1

| Age groups | Rule in arrondissement, provincial and relegation play-offs |
|---|---|
| Up to U13 | Straight to penalties |
| From U14 | Extra time (2 × 10 minutes), then penalties |

## Chapter 9: Launch (transition)

### Article 9.1: Structuring season
The **2025-26 season is played entirely under the old rules**. It is used to:
1. audit the Elite licences;
2. fix the geography (placements under Articles 2.3 and 2.4);
3. contract the federation travel;
4. set the calendar framework;
5. recruit and register the volunteer referees and referees in training (Chapter 10);
6. draw the 2026-27 tables.

### Article 9.2: Seeding for 2026-27
1. Every cohort **starts at the level it actually played in 2025-26**:

| Old level (2025-26) | New level (2026-27) |
|---|---|
| Elite | Elite 1 or Elite 2, subject to the licence |
| Interprovinciaal | IP |
| Provinciaal | P |
| Gewestelijk | G |

2. When a level has more teams than places, places go by the federation's 2025-26 ranking of those teams. **Teams that do not fit drop one level.**
3. When a level has fewer teams than places, it is **filled from the level below** by the same ranking.
4. The new U10 of 2026-27 is placed by **the entry rule** (Article 4.2).
5. The Elite 1 and Elite 2 split at launch follows where each academy **actually played in the real elite youth competitions in 2025-26**.
6. **Second and further teams** are seeded under **Article 4.12.1**, not by their 2025-26 level.
7. **Clubs holding an Elite licence for 2026-27** dissolve their second and further teams (Article 5.9).
8. **An academy that played Elite in 2025-26 but does not meet the sporting criterion** (Article 5.1) is treated as in **Article 5.7**: Voetbal Vlaanderen clubs enter IP as an extra team; FFA clubs enter the equivalent FFA level.

### Article 9.3: Launch cup
In the 2026-27 Youth Beker van België, cup qualification uses **the 2026-27 seeding order** instead of a previous-season ranking.

## Chapter 10: Refereeing

### Article 10.1: Gewestelijk
**A volunteer referee registered with Voetbal Vlaanderen**, provided by the home club. The away club can request a **neutral volunteer up to seven days before the match**.

### Article 10.2: Provinciaal
1. Volunteer referees, as in Article 10.1.
2. **Exception:** the **last two scheduled matchdays** of each table, and **every catch-up match played after the first of those two**, are refereed by **referees in training**.
3. When there are not enough referees in training for those matches, **official referees** cover them.

### Article 10.3: Interprovinciaal
All matches are refereed by **referees in training**.

### Article 10.4: Play-offs
**All play-off matches at every level** (arrondissement, provincial and relegation play-offs) are refereed by **referees in training**.

### Article 10.5: Youth Beker van België
1. Rounds 1 to 3: **referees in training**.
2. From round 4: referees from the **Elite system** (Article 10.6).

### Article 10.6: Elite 1 and Elite 2
Referees are **assigned by the KBVB referee department**, never provided by either club.

### Article 10.7: Definitions and minimum ages

| | Definition | Minimum age |
|---|---|---|
| **Referee in training** | Enrolled in the official Voetbal Vlaanderen referee course | **15** |
| **Volunteer referee** | Registered with Voetbal Vlaanderen after completing the online module | **16** |

### Article 10.8: No referee
1. If the assigned referee does not arrive, **any adult both coaches agree on** may referee.
2. If nobody can, it is **force majeure**: no forfeit, and the match is replayed on a Wednesday within three weeks (Article 3.4).

---

## Chapter 11: Everything else

### Article 11.1
All Voetbal Vlaanderen, KBVB and FFA regulations **not changed by this rulebook stay in force unchanged** (match sheets, eligibility, transfers, discipline, refereeing outside Chapter 10).
