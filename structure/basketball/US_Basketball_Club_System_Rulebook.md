# THE AMERICAN BASKETBALL CLUB SYSTEM
## Official Rulebook
### Announced in the 2022/23 season · Full system in play from 2026/27 · Implemented in stages until the system is established and stable · High School and College Pyramids from 2028/29 (Parts XVII–XVIII)

> One pyramid, from a county gym to the NBA Finals. Every club in a published division. Every player's path visible.

*World-building rulebook for simulation campaigns. Real leagues, clubs and institutions are used; their adoption of these rules is fictional.*

---

## PART I: GOVERNANCE

**Article 1. Governing bodies**
1. **The NBA** runs tiers 1 and 2 (NBA and G League), their academies and youth leagues.
2. **USA Basketball** runs tiers 3 and 4 (AAND and State Divisions), all national and state youth cups, licensing for tiers 3 and 4, and the official platform.
3. A **Joint Pyramid Board** (NBA and USA Basketball) rules on promotion and relegation between tiers 2 and 3, on transfers between tiers, and on disputes.

**Article 2. Federal assistance**
1. The **US federal government** assists the system financially and administratively, as set out in the Federal Basketball Pyramid Programme (Part XIV).
2. The government has no say in selection, results, promotion or relegation.

**Article 3. Age groups**
Age groups are set by **birth year**, not school grade.

---

## PART II: THE PYRAMID

**Article 4. The tiers**

| Tier | League | Clubs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NBA** | 30 |
| 2 | **G League** | 30 |
| 3 | **American Amateur National Division (AAND)** | 64: East, Midwest, South and West conferences of 16 |
| 4 | **State Divisions**: SD1 (statewide), SD2 (regional), SD3 (county or metro) | Open entry |

**Article 5. Tier 4 naming**
1. A division's name shows its level and where it plays: *[State] SD1* (the whole state), *[State] SD2 [Region]*, *[State] SD3 [County or metro area]*.
2. Small states share combined SD1 leagues: *Mountain SD1* (Montana, Idaho, Wyoming), *Dakotas SD1*, *Northern New England SD1* (Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont). Their SD2 and SD3 divisions remain within each state.
3. **Alaska and Hawaii** run their own leagues at every level.

**Article 6. Division sizes**
SD1 and SD2: 12–16 clubs. SD3: 8–16 clubs.

---

## PART III: TRANSPARENCY

**Article 7. The official platform**
1. Every division, club, fixture, result and table, from the NBA to the lowest SD3, is published on **one public platform and app**.
2. Each division's clubs are published by **1 July** and do not change during the season.
3. Every club's entry shows its name, home venue with address, colours, head coach and youth registration contact.
4. The home club enters each result within **24 hours**; tables update automatically.
5. No match that counts in a competition may be played outside a published division or cup.

---

## PART IV: LICENCES

**Article 8. Club licences**

| Licence | Required for | Key requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **NBA Club Licence** | Tier 1 | Arena of at least **15,000**; full academy U12–U19 with A teams; **women's teams from U16**; audited finances |
| **G League Club Licence** | Tier 2 | Arena of at least **4,000**; academy U12–U19 (A teams); women's teams from U16; finances covering a full season |
| **AAND Club Licence** | Tier 3 | Arena of at least **1,500**; academy with at least one team per age group U12–U19; **proof of national travel funding**; stable income; debt within set limits |
| Tier 4 | — | No club licence; registration with the state |

**Article 9. Academy grades**
1. Every academy in tiers 1–3 is graded **Platinum**, **Gold** or **Silver**, on coaching qualifications, facilities, medical staff, education support and first-team minutes for academy players.
2. Only **Platinum and Gold** academies may sign players from **outside their state before age 15**.

**Article 10. Promotion requires a licence**
A club may only be promoted if it holds the licence for the tier it is promoted to. If not, the next eligible club is promoted. If none is eligible, the club due to be relegated from that tier stays up.

---

## PART V: SENIOR COMPETITIONS

**Article 11. NBA (tier 1)**
1. **82 games**, October to April; East and West conferences; play-in and play-offs unchanged.
2. **The NBA Cup** is kept.
3. **Relegation:** the **two clubs with the worst regular-season records overall** are relegated to the G League.
4. **Parachute payments:** a relegated club receives a decreasing share of NBA central income for **two seasons**, or until it returns.
5. Salary cap and luxury tax are kept.

**Article 12. G League (tier 2)**
1. About **50 games**, November to April, in two conferences.
2. **Promotion:** the **regular-season champion** and the **play-off winner**, if independent and licensed. Otherwise the next eligible club.
3. **Relegation:** the bottom two clubs to the AAND.
4. **Affiliates:** clubs owned by or affiliated to an NBA club **cannot be promoted**. If the NBA parent is relegated, the affiliation ends.
5. Wages may not exceed **70% of the club's income**.

**Article 13. AAND (tier 3)**
1. Four conferences of 16; **30 games**, home and away within the conference, October to March.
2. **National play-offs:** the top 4 of each conference, a 16-club bracket in April.
3. **Promotion:** the **two best licensed clubs** in the national play-offs.
4. **Relegation:** the **bottom club of each conference**, replaced by **four regional play-off winners** from the SD1 leagues.
5. Contracts: amateur, semi-professional or professional.
6. Wages may not exceed **70% of income**.

**Article 14. State Divisions (tier 4)**
1. In each division, the **top 2 are promoted** and the **bottom 2 relegated**, with play-offs where divisions merge into fewer above.
2. **Promotion to the AAND:** finish in the top 2 of an SD1, hold an AAND licence, and win the regional play-off.

**Article 15. The American Basketball Cup**
1. Open to tiers 1–3 and the State SD1 champions.
2. Entry: SD1 champions and AAND clubs first; then the G League; then the NBA.
3. Single matches; the lower-tier club plays at home. Final in May.
4. Starts **when ready**, as decided by the Joint Pyramid Board.

---

## PART VI: ACADEMIES AND YOUTH COMPETITIONS

**Article 16. Academies**
1. **Every club in tiers 1–3 must have an academy**, with at least **one team per age group, U12 to U19**. B teams are recommended.
2. Tier 4 academies are optional.

**Article 17. Youth leagues**
1. Each tier runs its own youth leagues. **A club's youth team plays at the level it has earned**, which can be above or below its senior team's tier (17.4–17.18).
2. Tier 4 runs **State Youth Leagues** with the same levels and naming: Youth SD1, SD2, SD3.
3. **Youth divisions move on their own results**, not with the senior team's level.

*Amendment, November 2026 (user ruling): youth promotion and relegation through every tier.*

4. **Youth teams can go up from tier 4 to the AAND Academy League on results**, even if the club's senior team is in tier 4 or the club has no senior team.
5. **The route mirrors the senior one (Art. 14.2):** per age group, a team must **finish in the top 2 of its Youth SD1**, **its club must hold the AAND Club Licence (Art. 8)**, and it must **win the regional youth play-off**.
6. **The licence is the full AAND Club Licence**, with no youth-only version. The arena of at least 1,500 must be **owned or contracted as the club's home venue for the whole season**; hiring it for single games doesn't count. **Proof of national travel funding** must cover every team the club has in tier 1–3 youth leagues. The licence must be held by **1 April** to enter the play-off and must still be held on **1 July**, or the place goes to the next eligible team.
7. **Regional youth play-off:** one per **AAND conference** (East, Midwest, South, West) and **per age group**, between the eligible Youth SD1 teams of the states in that conference. Single games; the higher-placed team plays at home. **One team per conference and age group goes up.**
8. **Relegation keeps the AAND Academy League at 16 per conference:** in each age group, **the bottom team of each conference goes down** to the Youth SD1 of its state. **This applies to AAND clubs' academy teams too.** A relegated academy team still counts towards its club's Art. 16 obligation (a team per age group). If no eligible team goes up in a conference, the team due to go down stays up (as Art. 10).
9. **The place follows the cohort.** A team that goes up at U14 plays its **U15** season in the AAND Academy League, and so on up to U19. **There is no promotion out of U19.** A club's new **U12** starts at the level its previous U12 played, so a promoted cohort doesn't pull the age groups below it up.
10. **The whole youth ladder is open**, in both directions: Youth SD3 ↔ SD2 ↔ SD1 ↔ **AAND Academy League** ↔ **G League Academy League** ↔ **NBA Academy League** (17.14–17.18). Every step up needs **the licence of the tier the league belongs to**, held by the club whatever its senior tier.
11. **One team per club per age group** may play in a tier 1–3 youth league. A club's B team in the same age group stays in tier 4.
12. **A team follows the rules of the league it plays in, whatever its club's senior tier:** in any tier 1–3 youth league, **no pay-to-play**, directly or indirectly (membership fees, compulsory camps or travel charges for that team's players) (Art. 21), and **youth movement under Arts. 24–25** for its players; in the G League and NBA Academy Leagues, **every match is streamed** (Art. 20). **Training compensation (Art. 27) is paid at the rate of the league the player's new team plays in**: AAND, G League or NBA.
13. **The whole club becomes a graded academy (Art. 9)** from the season any of its teams plays in a tier 1–3 youth league. **Art. 9.2 then applies to all of the club's recruitment**, including its tier-4 teams: no signing from outside the state before 15 unless the academy is graded Platinum or Gold.
14. **AAND Academy League → G League Academy League.** Per age group, **two teams go up**: the **two best in a national youth play-off** between the four AAND conference winners, **if their clubs hold the G League Club Licence** (Art. 8: arena of 4,000, academy U12–U19 with A teams, women's teams from U16, finances covering a full season). Otherwise the next eligible team. **The bottom team of each G League Academy League conference goes down** to the AAND Academy League conference of its region.
15. **G League Academy League → NBA Academy League.** Per age group, **two teams go up**: the **best-placed eligible team in the regular season** and the **winner of the final four**, **if their clubs hold the NBA Club Licence** (arena of 15,000, full academy U12–U19 with A teams, women's teams from U16, audited finances). **The bottom team of each NBA Academy League conference goes down** to the G League Academy League.
16. **Affiliates (Art. 12.4) can't go up into the NBA Academy League**, the same as their senior teams. **An NBA club's academy team and its own affiliate's team may never play in the same youth league**: if a relegation would put them together, the affiliate's team takes the next lower place that keeps them apart, and the team below it stays up.
17. **Relegated NBA and G League academy teams** still count towards their club's Art. 16 obligation, keep their club's academy grade, and stay under the rules of the league they now play in (17.12).
18. **Timing.** Youth promotion and relegation at every level **runs from the 2026/27 season**, with the first moves for **2027/28**, **independently of the Joint Pyramid Board's timetable for senior promotion between tiers** (Art. 55). If no eligible team goes up, the team due to go down stays up (as Art. 10).

**Article 18. National Basketball Youth Cup (tiers 1–3)**
1. Age groups **U14 and older**.
2. Tier 3 academies play each other first; then tier 2 enters; then tier 1.

**Article 19. State Youth Cup (tier 4)**
1. Age groups **U14 and older**.
2. SD3 teams play each other first; then SD2 enters; then SD1.

**Article 20. Streaming**
1. From **U13**, every youth league match in **tiers 1 and 2** is streamed.
2. No streaming is required in tiers 3 and 4.
3. **All youth cup matches** are streamed.
4. Youth streaming rights are sold centrally and shared between academies, with a share reserved for education programmes.

**Article 21. Pay-to-play**
Charging players to play is **banned in tiers 1–3**. It is allowed in tier 4.

**Article 22. School basketball**
An academy player may also play **high school basketball** if his club allows it. Club matches are scheduled at **weekends** and high school games on **weekdays**.

**Article 22A. Playing up and senior eligibility** *(user ruling, November 2026)*
1. **Playing up in youth basketball.** A player may play for **any older age group** of his own club, without restriction. He can never play for a younger age group than his birth year allows (Art. 3).
2. **Senior basketball under 18.** A player under 18 may play senior games only if:

| Senior team | Requirements (boys) | Requirements (girls) |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-professional: tier 3 (AAND, including a university club's NCAA B team) and tier 4** | **14 or older** and **officially measured at 6'3" or taller** | 14 or older and 5'11" or taller |
| **Professional: tier 1 (NBA) and tier 2 (G League)** | **A professional contract**, **14 or older** and **officially measured at 6'5" or taller** | A professional contract, 14 or older and 6'1" or taller |

3. **From 18**, anyone may play senior basketball, without any height condition.
4. **Age** means the player's actual date of birth: he qualifies on his 14th birthday, not at the start of his birth year.
5. **Height** counts only when the league's medical staff have measured it, shoes off, **twice, six months apart** (Art. 33). A player who already qualified at 6'3" still needs two official measurements at or above 6'5" for professional teams.
6. **Senior games** means every senior game: league, cup, play-offs, pre-season, summer league and friendlies. **Practising** with a senior team is not restricted.
7. **One game a day.** A player may play at most one game per calendar day, across all his teams: youth, senior and high school (Art. 22).
8. **Cup-tied.** In a knockout competition, a player may play for only **one of his club's teams per season**.
9. **Post-season.** A player may play in a team's final four, play-off or promotion play-off only if he played at least **a quarter of that team's regular-season games**.
10. **Own club only.** A player may play up, or play senior games, only for the club he's registered with (Art. 23). The one exception is a loan, from 17 (Art. 30).
11. **League places stay with the cohort.** A player playing up doesn't change the league his own age group plays in (Art. 17.9).
12. **High school basketball** is not covered by this article; Art. 22 applies.

## PART VII: PLAYER MOVEMENT

**Article 23. Youth registration**
Players register with **one club at a time**.

**Article 24. U12–U14 (tiers 1–3)**
1. Players may move **freely each summer**.
2. The new club pays **training compensation** to the previous club.

**Article 25. U15 and older (tiers 1–3)**
1. Transfers are **negotiated** between clubs.
2. Transfer windows: **1 July–31 August** and **January**.

**Article 26. Tier 4**
Players move **freely each summer, without fees**. Training compensation applies only when a tier 1–3 club signs a tier 4 player.

**Article 27. Training compensation (per season trained)**

| Buying club's tier | Per season |
|---|---|
| NBA | **$150,000** |
| G League | **$50,000** |
| AAND | **$15,000** |

**Article 28. Players under professional contract**
A player under a professional contract can only move for a **negotiated fee**, at any age.

**Article 29. Senior transfers and trades**
1. Transfers with fees and **trades** are both allowed.
2. Windows: **1 July–31 August** and **January**.

**Article 30. Loans**
Loans are allowed from age **17**, including abroad. Minutes played on loan don't count for either club's homegrown rule.

**Article 31. The NBA draft**
**The NBA draft is abolished.**

---

## PART VIII: CONTRACTS

**Article 32. First professional contract**
1. At **14**, if the player is officially measured at **6'3" or taller** (boys) or **5'11" or taller** (girls).
2. Otherwise at **16**.

**Article 33. Height measurement**
Shoes off, by the league's medical staff, **twice, six months apart**.

**Article 34. Contracts for minors**
1. At most **three years** until 18.
2. **School attendance is compulsory.**
3. Most earnings are paid into a **trust** the player receives at 18.
4. A parent or legal guardian co-signs.

**Article 35. Agents**
Agents may approach a player only from **six months before he can sign a professional contract**: 13½ for players qualifying at 14, otherwise 15½.

---

## PART IX: THE HOMEGROWN RULE (TIERS 1 AND 2)

**Article 36. Who is an academy player**
1. A player trained at the club for at least **three seasons between ages 12 and 21**.
2. A **university club player signed before 21** also counts, if he then spends at least **two seasons** in the signing club's academy or first team.
3. At a **university club** (Part XVI), a student who has played for the club for **three seasons** counts as an academy player.

**Article 37. The 10% rule**
1. Each season, at least **two academy players** must play at least **10% of the first team's minutes combined** (about 1,970 minutes in the NBA; about 1,200 in the G League).
2. Only **men's first-team** minutes count. A separate rule for women's teams will follow.

**Article 38. Penalties**

| Breach | Penalty |
|---|---|
| 1st | **Points deduction** (in the NBA, wins deducted from the regular-season record) |
| 2nd consecutive season | **Transfer ban** |
| 3rd | **Relegation** |

---

## PART X: COLLEGE BASKETBALL

**Article 39. The NCAA continues**
1. The **NCAA continues** to run college basketball, in Divisions I, II and III, for every university that **has not become a club**, and for the **B teams of university clubs** (Article 64).
2. The **NCAA tournament (March Madness)** continues.
3. The NAIA and the NJCAA continue unchanged.
4. **From 2028/29, all college basketball is played in the College Pyramid (Part XVIII)**, which replaces points 1–3: the NCAA runs every tier centrally, and Divisions I, II and III, the NAIA and the NJCAA become levels inside it (Art. 81).

**Article 40. Pre-season matches**
Every pre-season, each **NCAA Division I team** and each **university club first team in the AAND or higher** plays **one to three NBA clubs**.

**Article 41. Signing college players**
1. A pyramid club may sign an NCAA player or a university club player **into its academy** or **straight into its first team**.
2. The university receives **training compensation at the buying club's tier rate** for each season the player trained there.
3. For a player on a **student contract** or a professional contract, the clubs negotiate a fee (Part VII), which may not be lower than that training compensation.

## PART XI: MOVING ABROAD

**Article 42. US academy players to clubs abroad**
1. From **13**, only if **at least one legal guardian** moves with the player; or from **18**, with an adult of the player's choice.
2. The receiving club must prove it will pay **travel, housing**, and, if the player hasn't finished school, **education**.

**Article 43. Foreign minors to US academies**
The same conditions apply in reverse.

**Article 44. Foreign players**
No quota for adults.

---

## PART XII: MIXED TEAMS AND WOMEN'S BASKETBALL

**Article 45. Mixed teams**
1. **Tiers 3 and 4:** mixed teams allowed **at every age, including seniors**.
2. **Tiers 1 and 2:** girls may play in boys' academy teams **up to and including U19**. At senior level, women play in the club's women's team.

**Article 46. Women's teams**
Tier 1 and 2 clubs must have women's teams **from U16 upwards**.

**Article 47. Interim competitions**
Until the women's system is designed: a **National Women's Academy League** (U16, U17, U19) and a **National Women's League** (seniors) for tier 1–2 clubs. The **WNBA** continues unchanged.

**Article 48. Facilities and travel**
Clubs with mixed teams provide separate changing rooms, and a female staff member on away trips with minors.

---

## PART XIII: FINANCE, COACHING AND SAFEGUARDING

**Article 49. Wages**
Tiers 2 and 3: wages may not exceed **70% of club income**. The NBA keeps its salary cap and luxury tax.

**Article 50. Solidarity**
A set share of NBA central television income is distributed to tiers 2–4: most to the G League, then the AAND, then State Division academies.

**Article 51. Coaching**
SD1 head coaches hold a national coaching certificate; SD2 and SD3 at least a basic certificate.

**Article 52. Safeguarding**
Every youth coach and staff member: background check and safeguarding training.

---

## PART XIV: THE FEDERAL BASKETBALL PYRAMID PROGRAMME

**Article 53. Financial assistance**
Federal grants for **academy facilities**, **gyms in underserved areas**, **travel funds for AAND and SD1 clubs**, and **education programmes** for academy players.

**Article 54. Administrative assistance**
A federal **Office for the Basketball Pyramid**, supporting:
1. the official platform and its data;
2. background checks for every youth coach;
3. coordination with states on youth labour rules for contracts at 14 and 16;
4. visas and education arrangements for players moving to and from the US.

Rules affecting NBA and G League players' contracts, including the end of the draft, take effect only once agreed with the **players' union** in the collective bargaining agreement.

## PART XVI: UNIVERSITY CLUBS

**Article 57. Who can apply**
1. Any university or college may apply to enter the pyramid with its **varsity basketball team as a club**, under the university's name.
2. Applications open in **spring 2026**, in the same licence round as other clubs.

**Article 58. Initial placement**
1. The best-licensed university clubs start in the **AAND**; the rest start in **SD1** of their state.
2. **No university club starts in the G League or the NBA.**
3. Placement is decided by the Joint Pyramid Board, on the club's licence and playing strength.

**Article 59. No ceiling**
A university club may be promoted to any tier, **including the NBA**, if it holds the required licence.

**Article 60. Money**
1. The **70% wage rule** applies to the university club's **basketball income only**.
2. **Tuition income, endowments and other sports budgets may not fund the club's wages.**
3. University clubs pay and receive training compensation and transfer fees like any club in their tier.

**Article 61. Academies**
1. A university club in tiers 1–3 must run its own **U17 and U19** teams.
2. It may meet the **U12–U16** requirement through **partner academies**: local schools or clubs running those age groups under its name.

**Article 62. Student contracts**
1. A university club may offer a **student contract**: paid, with tuition and housing included.
2. The player must stay **enrolled**, with **minimum credits each semester**, checked by the federal Office for the Basketball Pyramid.
3. If a player fails the requirement, his contract becomes a normal **semi-professional contract** for the rest of its term.
4. University clubs may also offer normal professional and semi-professional contracts.

**Article 63. Women's basketball**
1. A university club must fund **women's basketball to the same standard** as its men's club, in line with equal-treatment law.
2. University women's teams may enter the interim **National Women's League** (Article 47).

**Article 64. The compulsory NCAA B team**
1. **Every university club must have a B team playing in the NCAA.** This is a condition of its club licence.
2. The B team plays in the **NCAA division** the university belonged to before becoming a club. **From 2028/29 it is placed in the College Pyramid on merit**, like any other college team (Art. 83).
3. The B team is made up of **students outside the club's first-team squad**, mostly younger players.
4. Players may be moved between the first team and the B team during the season.
5. A player who plays **more than 10 pyramid matches** in a season may not play in that season's **NCAA tournament**. *(From 2028/29 the College Pyramid's matches don't count as pyramid matches; only club matches do.)*
6. A university club without an NCAA B team **loses its club licence** and is placed in the lowest State Division level of its state the following season.

## PART XVII: THE HIGH SCHOOL PYRAMID
*Added November 2026 (user ruling). Announced in **2026/27**; first season **2028/29**. Boys' varsity basketball.*

**Article 65. Governing bodies**
1. The **NFHS** runs **tiers 1–3** of the High School Pyramid, their licences and the National High School Cup.
2. Each **state association** runs **tier 4** in its state.
3. Disputes between tiers go to an **NFHS appeals panel**, whose decision is final.

**Article 66. The tiers** *(the club pyramid's shape, Art. 4)*

| Tier | League | Schools |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **National High School League (NHSL)** | **30**: East and West conferences of 15 |
| 2 | **NHSL 2** | **30**: two conferences of 15 |
| 3 | **High School National Division (HSND)** | **64**: East, Midwest, South and West conferences of 16 |
| 4 | **State HS Divisions**: **HS SD1** (statewide), **HS SD2** (regional), **HS SD3** (county or metro) | Open entry |

1. Tier 4 follows the naming, small-state combinations and division sizes of **Art. 5 and 6** (*[State] HS SD1*, *Mountain HS SD1*, and so on). Alaska and Hawaii run their own levels.
2. **Enrolment classes are abolished.** A school plays at the level its varsity earns.

**Article 67. Who plays**
1. **One varsity per school.** A school is one registration with its state association, whatever campuses, programmes or teams it runs under its name. *(Closure: no "national" and "select" varsities of the same school.)*
2. **Junior varsity is the school's B team.** It plays **only in tier 4**, at least **two levels below** its varsity, and never in the same division. It can't be promoted into tiers 1–3.
3. **Players:** full-time students of that school, in **grades 9 to 12**, at most **four seasons** of varsity, and **under 19 on 1 September** of the season. *(Closure: no fifth-year or post-graduate varsity players.)*
4. **One school per season.** A player who has played a varsity game for one school can't play for another school's varsity in the same season. *(Closure: no mid-season school hopping.)*
5. **Prep schools** enter like any other school.
6. **Academy players** may play if their club allows (Art. 22); one game a day applies (Art. 22A.7).

**Article 68. The season**
1. **Weekdays only** (Art. 22): club basketball keeps the weekends.
2. Every tier plays a **full-season league**: tiers 1–2 home and away within the conference (**28 games**), tier 3 home and away within the conference (**30 games**), tier 4 as Art. 6.
3. **Every division, fixture, result and table is on the official platform** (Art. 7).

**Article 69. Hub events (tiers 1–3)**
1. Tier 1–3 games are played at **hub events**: several schools of one conference meet at one venue, each school playing **one game a day**.
2. Hubs are held on **Fridays** and in **school holidays** (Thanksgiving, the winter break, Presidents' Day week), set by the NFHS before the season.
3. Every game keeps a **home team**, and **each school is home in half its games**. A hub venue must meet the **home team's licence** (Art. 72).
4. The **NFHS pays travel and accommodation** for every hub. *(Closure: no school can be priced out of its tier.)*
5. **Students miss at most two school days per hub**; the NFHS sets hub dates so that no student misses more than **ten school days a season** for tier 1–3 games.

**Article 70. Promotion and relegation** *(the club rules, Art. 11–14)*

| Tier | Up | Down |
|---|---|---|
| **NHSL (1)** | — | The **two schools with the worst records overall** |
| **NHSL 2 (2)** | The **regular-season champion** and the **play-off winner** | The **bottom two** |
| **HSND (3)** | The **two best licensed schools** in the **national play-offs** (top 4 of each conference, a 16-school bracket) | The **bottom school of each conference** |
| **HS SD1 → HSND** | A school in the **top 2 of its HS SD1**, with the **HSND licence**, that wins its **regional play-off** (one per HSND conference; four up) | — |
| **HS SD1–SD3** | The **top two** | The **bottom two** |

1. **A school may only go up with the licence** of the tier above (Art. 10 applies: otherwise the next eligible school; if none, the school due to go down stays up).
2. **Timing:** the first season is **2028/29**; the first moves apply in **2029/30**.

**Article 71. Champions**
1. **National high school champion:** the winner of the **NHSL final four** (the top two of each conference), at a neutral venue in March.
2. **State champion:** the **HS SD1 champion** of each state (or of each combined SD1, Art. 66.1).

**Article 72. Licences** *(the club licences, Art. 8)*

| Licence | Gym | Other |
|---|---|---|
| **NHSL** | **3,000 seats** | **Certified head coach**; the school's academic standard (Art. 72.2) |
| **NHSL 2** | **2,000 seats** | As above |
| **HSND** | **1,000 seats** | Certified head coach |
| Tier 4 | — | Registration with the state association |

1. The gym must be **owned by the school or contracted as its home gym for the whole season**; hiring it for single games doesn't count (as Art. 17.6). Travel is covered by the NFHS (Art. 69.4), so no travel funding is required.
2. **Academic standard:** every varsity player must meet his **state's academic eligibility rules**, checked by the NFHS **each semester**. A player who fails is out until he meets them again.
3. The licence must be held by **1 April** to enter a play-off and still on **1 July**.

**Article 73. The National High School Cup** *(the American Basketball Cup, Art. 15)*
1. Open to **tiers 1–3** and the **HS SD1 champions**.
2. Entry: **HS SD1 champions and HSND schools** first; then **NHSL 2**; then **NHSL**.
3. **Single games; the lower-tier school plays at home**, at a hub if the distance needs one. Final in March.
4. **Varsities only.**

## PART XVIII: THE COLLEGE PYRAMID
*Added November 2026 (user ruling). Announced in **2026/27**; first season **2028/29**. Men's college basketball; women's college basketball continues under the NCAA's existing rules until it is designed.*

**Article 75. Governing body**
1. The **NCAA runs every tier centrally**: competitions, scheduling, officiating, eligibility, licences and **transfer windows**.
2. **Media rights** for every tier and for March Madness are **sold centrally by the NCAA** and shared out **by tier**, with a set share for every tier, including tier 4 (as Art. 50).
3. **Conferences no longer run competitions.** They may continue as associations of universities, without leagues, tournaments or media deals of their own.

**Article 76. The tiers** *(the club pyramid's shape, Art. 4)*

| Tier | League | Teams |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NCAA National League** | **30**: East and West conferences of 15 |
| 2 | **NCAA National League 2** | **30**: two conferences of 15 |
| 3 | **NCAA National Division** | **64**: East, Midwest, South and West conferences of 16 |
| 4 | **College State Divisions**: **College SD1** (statewide), **College SD2** (regional), **College SD3** (county or metro) | Every other college team |

Tier 4 follows the naming, small-state combinations and division sizes of **Art. 5 and 6**.

**Article 77. Who plays**
1. **One team per university or college** in the College Pyramid:
   1. the **first team** of a university that is not a club (Art. 39);
   2. the **B team** of a university club (Art. 64), which is that university's college team.
2. **Players:** enrolled students who meet the NCAA's academic and eligibility rules, checked centrally **each semester**.
3. A player moving from a university club's first team to its B team plays for the B team **from the next game**; Art. 64.4 and 64.5 still apply.

**Article 78. The season**
1. **November to early March**, then March Madness.
2. Tiers 1–2: home and away within the conference (**28 games**); tier 3: home and away within the conference (**30 games**); tier 4: as Art. 6.
3. The pre-season games against NBA clubs (Art. 40) stay, for every tier 1–3 team.
4. **Everything is on the official platform** (Art. 7).

**Article 79. Promotion and relegation** *(the club rules, Art. 11–14, fitted to March Madness)*

| Tier | Up | Down |
|---|---|---|
| **National League (1)** | — | The **two teams with the worst records overall** |
| **National League 2 (2)** | The **regular-season champion** and **the tier-2 team that goes furthest in March Madness** | The **bottom two** |
| **National Division (3)** | The **two tier-3 teams that go furthest in March Madness** | The **bottom team of each conference** |
| **College SD1 → National Division** | The **winner of each regional qualifier** (Art. 80.3): four up | — |
| **College SD1–SD3** | The **top two** | The **bottom two** |

1. **Only licensed teams go up** (Art. 10 applies).
2. **"Furthest in March Madness":** ties (two teams out in the same round) go to the **better national ranking** on Selection Sunday.
3. **Timing:** the first season is **2028/29**; the first moves apply in **2029/30**.

**Article 80. March Madness**
1. **68 teams**, single elimination, the **First Four**, Selection Sunday, the Final Four: **all kept**.
2. **Who qualifies:**

| From | Places |
|---|---|
| **National League (1)** | **20**: the **top 10 of each conference** |
| **National League 2 (2)** | **12**: the **top 6 of each conference** |
| **National Division (3)** | **16**: the **top 4 of each conference** |
| **College SD1s (4)** | **20**: through **four regional qualifiers**, one per National Division conference (East, Midwest, South, West), between the SD1 champions of the states in that region, **five places per region** |
| **Total** | **68** |

3. **Regional qualifiers:** single games in early March; the qualifier's **winner** also earns promotion (Art. 79).
4. **The First Four** is played by the **eight tier-4 qualifiers with the lowest national ranking**.
5. **Seeding:** by the **NCAA's national ranking (NET)**, in an **S-curve over four regions**. No selection committee.
6. **Eligibility:** a player must have played **at least a quarter of his team's regular-season games** (as Art. 22A.9). Art. 64.5 still applies. *(Closure: no strengthening a B team with first-team players for March only.)*

**Article 81. Divisions II and III, the NAIA and the NJCAA**
1. From 2028/29 their teams play in the **College State Divisions**, at the level their placement or results give them.
2. **Their separate national championships end.** Every college team's route is the College Pyramid and March Madness.
3. Each institution keeps its own **scholarship and financial-aid rules**.

**Article 82. National League champion**
The team with the **best regular-season record in the National League** is **National League champion**, a separate title from March Madness. Ties go to head-to-head, then the national ranking.

**Article 83. Licences** *(the club licences, Art. 8)*

| Licence | Arena | Other |
|---|---|---|
| **National League** | **10,000 seats** | Audited finances; **women's basketball funded to the same standard** (as Art. 63); a team **Academic Progress Rate of 930 or higher** |
| **National League 2** | **5,000 seats** | As above |
| **National Division** | **2,500 seats** | Proof of **national travel funding**; APR 930 or higher |
| Tier 4 | — | Registration with the NCAA |

1. The arena must be **owned or contracted as the team's home for the whole season** (as Art. 17.6).
2. The licence must be held by **1 March** to go up and still on **1 July**.
3. **Initial placement and every promotion depend on the licence**; a university club's B team uses the university's own arena and finances, **counted separately from the club's** (Art. 60).

**Article 84. Launch**

Played under the old rules, with the old conference tournaments and the old March Madness selection |
