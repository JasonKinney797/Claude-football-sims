## Chapter 1: Scope and definitions

### Article 1.1: Scope
This rulebook governs all **boys' youth competitions of Voetbal Vlaanderen from U6 up to and including U19**, the national Elite youth competitions and the Youth Beker van België. Girls who play in boys' teams do so under the existing Voetbal Vlaanderen dispensation rules.

### Article 1.2: Age groups
Every age group is **one birth year** (U6, U7, U8 ... U19). **U19 is the last youth age group.**

### Article 1.3: Cohort
A cohort is a club's team in one age group. Each season the cohort **moves up one age group** (the U12 of this season is the U13 of next season). **The competitive level belongs to the cohort, not to the age group.**

### Article 1.4: Levels

| Rank | Level | Short | Age groups |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Elite 1 | E1 | **U13 to U19** |
| 2 | Elite 2 | E2 | **U13 to U19** |
| 3 | Interprovinciaal | IP | U10 to U19 |
| 4 | Provinciaal | P | U10 to U19 |
| 5 | Gewestelijk | G | U6 to U19 |

1. Within one level there can be **parallel tables** (table A, table B, and so on). Tables at the same level are equal in strength; the letter carries no rank.
2. **U6 to U9 play Gewestelijk.** Exception: where a club's U10 plays Provinciaal or Interprovinciaal, that club's U6 to U9 may play at that level too.
3. **Professional clubs below Elite age:** a professional club (Article 1.9) plays its **U10 to U12 in Interprovinciaal** and its **U6 to U9 in Gewestelijk**. These U10–U12 teams **take IP places by right**: they are placed in IP before any ranking is applied, inside the two tables of 14, and they are never relegated.

### Article 1.5: Match formats

| Format | Age groups |
|---|---|
| 2v2 | **Abolished** |
| 5v5 | U6 to U8 |
| 8v8 | U9 to U13 |
| 11v11 | U14 to U19 |

### Article 1.6: Standings
Standings are kept **from U10 onwards**, at every level from Gewestelijk up. U6 to U9 play without standings.

### Article 1.7: Registered home ground
Every club registers **one home ground per season**. All distances in this rulebook are measured from it. A club can change its registered home ground **only between seasons**.

### Article 1.8: Distance and "closer"
1. All distances are **crow-flies** from the registered home ground, unless an article says otherwise.
2. **"Closer"** (Articles 2.3 and 2.4) means the **average crow-flies distance** from the club's registered home ground to the other teams of the table it would join.

### Article 1.9: Professional club *(new in v1.5)*
1. A **professional club** is a club whose **senior first team plays in the Jupiler Pro League or the Challenger Pro League in the current season**. A club's U23 team in the Challenger Pro League does not count as its senior team.
2. Professional status is fixed **on the first day of the season** and holds for the whole season.
3. Professional status is checked per **club registration number (stamnummer)**. It covers **every entity under which that club's youth teams play**, including a separate youth non-profit, an academy company or a partnership structure.
4. Professional status decides **travel** (Article 5.10), **second teams** (Article 5.9) and **entry at U13** (Article 4.2a). It does **not** decide the level a team plays at.

### Article 1.10: Elite team *(new in v1.5)*
An **Elite team** is a cohort playing in Elite 1 or Elite 2 that season, whether its club is professional or not.

## Chapter 2: Geography

### Article 2.1: Arrondissements

| Province | Arrondissements |
|---|---|
| Antwerpen | Antwerpen, Mechelen, Turnhout |
| Limburg | Hasselt, Maaseik, Tongeren |
| Oost-Vlaanderen | Aalst, Dendermonde, Eeklo, Gent, Oudenaarde, Sint-Niklaas |
| Vlaams-Brabant | Halle-Vilvoorde, Leuven, plus Brussel (Article 2.2) |
| West-Vlaanderen | Brugge, Diksmuide, Ieper, Kortrijk, Oostende, Roeselare, Tielt, Veurne |

### Article 2.2: Brussels
For Voetbal Vlaanderen clubs, the **Brussels-Capital Region counts as an arrondissement of Vlaams-Brabant**.

### Article 2.3: Gewestelijk scope
1. A Gewestelijk table only contains teams from **the same arrondissement**, or from **municipalities directly bordering that arrondissement** in another arrondissement.
2. A club in a border municipality belongs to its own arrondissement, unless the federation places it in the neighbouring arrondissement's Gewestelijk because that is closer (Article 1.8).
3. That placement is **fixed for the whole season**, and the club then belongs to that arrondissement for the Gewestelijk play-offs and promotion.

### Article 2.4: Provinciaal scope
Provinciaal covers **the whole province plus exactly two bordering arrondissements of another province**. Each border arrondissement is claimed by one province only:

| Province | Claims |
|---|---|
| West-Vlaanderen | Eeklo, Oudenaarde |
| Oost-Vlaanderen | Tielt, Halle-Vilvoorde |
| Antwerpen | Sint-Niklaas, Dendermonde |
| Vlaams-Brabant | Mechelen, Hasselt |
| Limburg | Turnhout, Leuven |

1. A club in a claimed arrondissement plays in its own province's Provinciaal **by default**.
2. The federation can place it in the claiming province's Provinciaal when that is closer (Article 1.8).
3. A placed club **counts entirely for the province it plays in**: tables, play-offs, quotas, promotion and relegation.
4. The placement is fixed per season.

### Article 2.5: Interprovinciaal scope
Interprovinciaal covers **all five Flemish provinces plus the Voetbal Vlaanderen clubs of Brussels**. IP tables are drawn **fully mixed**, without geographic grouping.

### Article 2.6: Elite scope
Elite 1 and Elite 2 are **national competitions under the KBVB** and include clubs of both wings.

## Chapter 3: Season and calendar

### Article 3.1: One series per season
Every level plays **one full-season series**. There are no autumn or spring series.

### Article 3.2: Saturday
League matches are played on **Saturdays**, one match per team per Saturday.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kick-off window** | 09:00 to 18:00 |
| **Matches with travel of 50 km or more** (Article 6.1) | Not before 11:00 |

**Exception for U6 to U9** (no standings): matches may also be played on **Sunday mornings**.

### Article 3.3: Wednesday afternoon
Wednesday afternoons are reserved for:
1. the **six fixed cup Wednesdays** of the Youth Beker van België;
2. **catch-up and emergency** league matches, on the other Wednesdays.

### Article 3.4: Postponements and forfeits
1. A postponed league match is replayed on a Wednesday afternoon **within three weeks**.
2. If it is not played by then, **the team that caused the postponement loses by forfeit**.
3. **No forfeit for force majeure:** weather, an absent referee (Article 10.8), an unplayable pitch through no fault of the club, or a failure of federation transport (Article 6.6). The match is replayed within three weeks.
4. A forfeit is only given **for the club's own fault**.

### Article 3.5: Pairing of age groups
Clubs and fixture planners pair consecutive age groups so that, where possible, **one is at home when the other plays away** (for example U10 at home while U11 plays away).

### Article 3.6: Table sizes

| Level | Size | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Elite 1 | **16 teams** per age group | Double round robin |
| Elite 2 | **Target: 16 teams** per age group (Article 4.8.4 while below target) | **Double round robin with ten or more teams; quadruple round robin with fewer than ten** |
| Interprovinciaal | **Two tables of 14** per age group | Double round robin |
| Provinciaal | **Tables of 14**; number of tables per province fixed each season by the federation (Article 4.6.5) | Double round robin |
| Gewestelijk | **At most 14 teams per table**; splits into parallel tables when one table is not enough | Double round robin |

### Article 3.6a: Elite size correction *(new in v1.5)*
1. If an Elite table would start a season with **more than 16 teams**, it plays with all of them, **up to 18**. At the end of that season it relegates as many **extra** teams as needed to return to 16.
2. If it would start with **more than 18**, the teams beyond 18 start one level lower, chosen by the previous season's ranking of those cohorts (lowest first).
3. If an Elite table would start with **fewer than its target**, it is filled from the level below, **licensed teams only**, by the previous season's ranking of those cohorts (points per match). For a U13 table the ranking used is the club's **outgoing U12 cohort** for Elite 2, and the club's **outgoing U13 cohort** for Elite 1.
4. Elite 2 below target follows Article 4.8.4 instead of point 3.

### Article 3.7: Points and ranking
**Win 3, draw 1, loss 0.** Ranking order:
1. points
2. matches won
3. goal difference
4. goals scored
5. away goals scored
6. away matches won

When tables of different sizes must be compared, **points per match** is used first.

### Article 3.8: Play-off window and cup end dates
1. **Play-off window:** the **three Saturdays after the last league matchday**, for the arrondissement, provincial and relegation play-offs.
2. **Cup semi-finals:** the Saturday after the play-off window. **Cup final:** one Saturday later (Article 7.3).

## Chapter 4: Promotion and relegation

### Article 4.1: Cohort principle
A level earned at the end of a season in age group N applies to **the same cohort in age group N+1** the following season.

> **Example:** a U10 team that wins promotion to Provinciaal plays Provinciaal as U11. A U12 team that wins promotion to Elite 2 plays Elite 2 as U13.

### Article 4.2: Entry rule for U10
A club's new U10 **starts at the level the club's U11 plays that same season**. That is the level the previous U10 cohort earned. A professional club's new U10 starts in IP (Article 1.4.3).

### Article 4.2a: Entry into Elite at U13
1. **Non-professional clubs:** the U12 cohort moves up **at the level it earned**, like any other cohort (Article 4.1). It reaches Elite at U13 only by promotion (Article 4.8).
2. **Professional clubs:** the new U13 enters at **the higher** of:
   - the level the club's **outgoing U13 cohort played in** this season; or
   - the level the **U12 cohort earned** (Article 4.8).
3. A professional club whose outgoing U13 cohort did not play Elite, and whose U12 did not earn promotion, has its new U13 in **IP**.

### Article 4.3: Last age group
Promotion or relegation earned in **U19 lapses**. Promotion or relegation earned in U13 carries into 11v11 at U14 as normal.

### Article 4.4: Gewestelijk to Provinciaal
1. Per arrondissement and per age group, **the top two of every Gewestelijk table** go to the arrondissement play-offs, within the play-off window (Article 3.8).
2. The play-offs are a **single knockout**. When the number of teams is not a power of two, table winners get byes, best points per match first.
3. The better-ranked team plays at home; the final is played at a **neutral venue** named by the provincial committee.
4. **The winner is promoted** to Provinciaal.

### Article 4.5: Provinciaal to Interprovinciaal
1. Per province and per age group, **the top two of every Provinciaal table** go to the provincial play-offs, within the play-off window.
2. Single knockout, with byes for the best table winners when needed. **The two semi-final winners are promoted** to Interprovinciaal. The final decides the provincial title only.
3. Result: **two promotions per province, ten per age group** across Flanders.

### Article 4.6: Provinciaal relegation
1. Per province, the number of relegated teams equals **the number needed to bring every Provinciaal table back to 14** next season, **with a minimum of two**.
2. **Relegation play-off:** the 13th and 14th of every table are cross-paired against teams from other tables (13th of one table against 14th of another). The better-ranked team plays at home. **Losers are relegated.**
3. If more relegations are needed than there are losers, the lowest-ranked remaining teams are relegated by points per match, starting with the 12th places.
4. If fewer relegations are needed than there are losers, the losers with the best points per match are reprieved, never going below the minimum of two.
5. **The federation fixes next season's number of Provinciaal tables per province by 1 March** at the latest.

### Article 4.7: Interprovinciaal relegation
1. **The bottom five of each IP table are relegated**: ten per age group.
2. If more relegations are needed to keep IP at 28 teams (for example after relegations from Elite 2), the next lowest teams by points per match are relegated as well. If fewer are needed, the relegated teams with the best points per match are reprieved.
3. A relegated team goes to the Provinciaal of the province it belongs to, including a placement made under Article 2.4.
4. A professional club's U10–U12 teams are never relegated (Article 1.4.3).
5. **U10–U12** *(A43)*: a non-professional team is relegated only if it finishes **in the bottom five places of its table**, professional clubs counted in the positions. A non-professional team finishing above those places stays up, even if fewer than five teams go down.
6. **Promotions into U10–U12 IP** *(A43)*: the number of Provinciaal teams promoted equals **the places free** for non-professional cohorts (28 minus the professional clubs, minus the non-professional teams staying up). If more teams earn promotion under Article 4.5, the provincial semi-final winners are ranked **by points per match** and the best go up; the others stay in Provinciaal and are first in line for vacancies (Article 4.11).

### Article 4.8: Interprovinciaal to Elite 2 *(replaced in v1.5)*
1. **Promotion:** per age group from U12 to U18, **the winner of each IP table is promoted to Elite 2**, under the cohort principle. The FFA's equivalent interprovincial level promotes **one team** per age group, its champion.
2. **Eligibility:** a promoted team must hold an **Elite licence** for the coming season (Chapter 5). Not eligible:
   - a team without a licence;
   - a professional club's U12 (it enters U13 under Article 4.2a, which already takes its promotion into account);
   - a second team (Article 4.12.4).
3. **Passing down:** when a table winner is not eligible or declines (Article 4.10), the place goes to **the next-best eligible team in the same table**, down to fourth place. If none of the top four is eligible, it goes to **the other IP table's best eligible team not yet promoted, provided that team also finished in its table's top four** *(A44)*. If nobody takes it, the place stays empty and Elite 2 relegates one team fewer.
4. **Elite 2 below target:** while an Elite 2 age group has **fewer than 16 teams**, it **relegates no teams** to IP or the FFA level, and the promotions in point 1 are added on top. Elite 2 then grows by up to three teams per season until it reaches 16.
5. **Relegation:** once an Elite 2 age group is at 16, **the bottom three are relegated** each season. Voetbal Vlaanderen clubs go to **IP**, into the table with the fewest teams; FFA clubs go to the FFA's equivalent level.
6. **U19:** nothing is earned in U19 (Article 4.3).

### Article 4.9: Elite 1 and Elite 2
1. Per age group, **the bottom two of Elite 1 drop to Elite 2** and **the top two of Elite 2 move up to Elite 1**, under the cohort principle. A team moving up must hold an Elite licence.
2. **Elite 1 vacancy:** filled by the **best-placed licensed Elite 2 team that didn't move up**.

### Article 4.10: Accepting or declining promotion
1. A club accepts promotion **in writing within seven days after the play-off final** (for promotion to Elite 2: within seven days after the last IP matchday). **No answer counts as a denial.**
2. A declined spot passes:
   - **into Provinciaal:** to the play-off runner-up, then to the other play-off participants in order of points per match;
   - **into Interprovinciaal:** to **the losing semi-finalists, by points per match**, then to the other play-off participants in order of points per match;
   - **into Elite 2:** under Article 4.8.3.
3. If nobody takes it, the spot stays empty and that level relegates one team fewer.

### Article 4.11: Vacancies
A club that cannot field a cohort in the next season **loses its spot**. The spot goes to the next team in line: first the best-placed team that missed promotion in the play-offs of the level below, then a reprieved relegated team. **In Elite, only licensed teams** can fill a vacancy.

### Article 4.12: Second and further teams (non-professional clubs)
1. **Starting level:** a second team starts **two levels below its club's first team in the same age group, but never below Gewestelijk**.

| First team | Second team starts in |
|---|---|
| Elite 1 or Elite 2 | **No second team** (point 7) |
| Interprovinciaal | Gewestelijk |
| Provinciaal | Gewestelijk |
| Gewestelijk | Gewestelijk, in a different table |

2. **Third and further teams** start in Gewestelijk. A club may enter **as many Gewestelijk teams as it wants**.
3. **Never in the same table** as another team of the same club, at any level.
4. A second team **can be promoted up to its first team's level**, and then plays in a **separate table** at that level. **A second team can never be promoted into Elite.**
5. **The team at the higher level is always the first team.** If a second team ends a season above its first team, the designations **swap at the end of the season**.
6. Second and further teams **do not play in the Youth Beker van België**.
7. **No second teams beside an Elite team** *(new in v1.5)*: a club whose team plays Elite in an age group has **no other team in that age group**. When a cohort is promoted to Elite, the club's other teams of that cohort are **dissolved at the end of the season**; their players are free to change club in the normal transfer window. If the Elite team is later relegated to IP, the club may enter a second team again under point 1.

### Article 4.13: Squad protection
1. During **the last five matchdays** of the season and during the play-offs, no player can move **between any two teams of the same club** in the same age group, **in either direction**.
2. From U6 up to and including U13, **every player on the match sheet plays at least half of the match**.
3. The existing Voetbal Vlaanderen **dispensation rules** (late developers, girls) remain in force unchanged.

### Article 4.14: Mergers

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Any merger | The merged club takes **the higher of the two levels** in every age group |
| The two clubs played at different levels | The second team **keeps the lower level** |
| The two clubs played at the same level | The second team **starts one level lower** |

A merged club that is **professional** keeps **one team per age group only** (Article 5.9). A merged club with an Elite team in an age group keeps only that team in that age group (Article 4.12.7).

### Article 4.15: No feeder Elite teams *(new in v1.5)*
1. A player who has played an official match for a **professional club's** team in a season **cannot play for another club's Elite team** in the same season.
2. A player cannot play for **two different clubs' Elite teams** in the same season.
3. **No loans** into or out of an Elite team during the season. Transfers in the normal windows are unaffected, subject to points 1 and 2.

## Chapter 5: Elite licence and professional clubs *(rewritten in v1.5)*

### Article 5.1: What the licence is for
1. **Every Elite team needs a licence.** The licence is issued **per club, per season**, and covers all of the club's Elite teams.
2. The licence **does not give a place in Elite**. Places are earned on the pitch (Articles 4.8 and 4.9), or by entry at U13 for professional clubs (Article 4.2a).
3. A club may apply for a licence **in any season**, whether or not it has an Elite team yet.

### Licence criteria

| Criterion | Professional club, or any club with 4+ Elite teams | Non-professional club with 1–3 Elite teams |
|---|---|---|
| **Pitches** (Art. 5.2) | At least **two full-size synthetic pitches and one full-size natural grass pitch** | At least **one full-size synthetic pitch and one full-size natural grass pitch** |
| **Medical** (Art. 5.3) | At least **two full-time medical staff**, at least one a physiotherapist, with a sports doctor reachable on every match day | A **qualified physiotherapist present at every Elite match and at least two Elite training sessions a week**, with a sports doctor reachable on every match day |
| **Buses** (Art. 5.4) | **One team bus per Elite age group** (professional clubs only) | Not required: federation travel applies (Chapter 6) |

### Article 5.2: Pitches
Pitches must be **owned or leased for at least a full season** and **used exclusively by the club**. Renting pitches from another club for the audit does not count.

### Article 5.3: Medical
Staff must be employed or contracted for the full season. A physiotherapist shared with the club's senior team counts.

### Article 5.4: Buses
For professional clubs: owned or leased for at least a full season, used exclusively by that age group. **A bus cannot be shared between two age groups.**

### Article 5.5: Audit, deadlines and appeal
1. **Clubs with an Elite team** are audited every season.
2. **Clubs in contention for promotion:** any club with a team in the **top four of an IP table on 1 March** may apply by **15 March** for a licence for the next season.
3. Decisions are published **by 30 April**. A licence granted for promotion is valid **only if the promotion happens**.
4. Appeals go to the **Belgian Court of Arbitration for Sport (BAS)**.
5. The criteria column that applies is set by the number of Elite teams the club **will have next season**, counting promotions it can still earn.

### Article 5.6: Loss of professional status
1. A club that is no longer professional **keeps every team at the level it earned**, provided it holds a licence under the non-professional criteria (it then needs no buses).
2. From that season, its teams are **covered by federation travel** (Chapter 6), and it may field second teams in age groups **without an Elite team** (Article 4.12).
3. If it does not meet the non-professional criteria, Article 5.7 applies.

### Article 5.7: Loss of licence
1. A club that fails the audit or the appeal **loses its Elite teams' places at the end of the season**.
2. Every one of its Elite teams **enters IP as an extra team** the following season, in **the IP table with the fewest teams**. IP relegates the same number of extra teams that season, so the table sizes are back on target a year later. FFA clubs enter the FFA's equivalent level.
3. In the Youth Beker van België, these teams **enter at the IP stage** (round 2).
4. **The way back is on the pitch**, through Article 4.8. There is no direct return.
5. Elite 1 and Elite 2 fill the vacancies under Articles 3.6a, 4.9.2 and 4.11.

### Article 5.8: Becoming professional
1. A club that becomes professional **keeps every team at the level it plays**. It gets no Elite place for it.
2. Its second and further teams are **dissolved at the end of the season before its first professional season**; their players are free to change club in the normal transfer window.
3. From its first professional season, its U10–U12 play IP by right (Article 1.4.3), its new U13 enters under Article 4.2a, and it needs the professional criteria for any Elite team.

### Article 5.8a: Status at the start of the season
Professional status is fixed on the first day of the season (Article 1.9.2). A club relegated out of professional football **before** the season begins is non-professional for that season, and is audited under the non-professional criteria.

### Article 5.9: One team per age group (professional clubs)
A professional club fields **exactly one team per age group, from U6 to U19**, at whatever level each cohort plays. It has **no second or further teams** at any level.

### Article 5.10: Travel
1. A professional club's **U13 to U19 teams** are **excluded from federation travel coverage at every level and in every competition**, including when they play IP. The club arranges and pays its own transport.
2. A professional club's **U6 to U12 teams are covered** like any other team.
3. A non-professional club's **Elite teams are covered** under Chapter 6, like every other team.

## Chapter 6: Federation travel

### Article 6.1: Principle
Every match that requires travel of **50 km or more, measured as the crow flies** from the travelling team's registered home ground to the venue, is **fully paid by the RBFA**.

| Applies to | Does not apply to |
|---|---|
| Every level: Gewestelijk, Provinciaal, Interprovinciaal, **Elite 1 and Elite 2** | **A professional club's U13–U19 teams** (Article 5.10) |
| League matches, play-offs, cup matches | Trips under 50 km, **except from cup round 3** (Article 7.6) |
| Both teams at a neutral venue | |

### Article 6.2: Who pays and who organises
1. **The RBFA pays.**
2. **Voetbal Vlaanderen** (and the **FFA** for its clubs) **organises and contracts** the transport: bus, train or any other means.

### Article 6.3: Door to door
1. Transport **picks the team up at its registered home ground** at a scheduled time.
2. It brings the team to a drop-off point **no more than 10 minutes' walk (about 800 m) from the dressing rooms** at the venue. If a bus cannot get that close, **the federation arranges a shuttle**.
3. **The return trip is included**: pickup at the same point, **no later than one hour after the final whistle**, back to the registered home ground.
4. The coach receives **the full travel details one week before the match**.

### Article 6.4: Who travels
**The players on the match sheet and up to four staff members. Parents are not covered.**

### Article 6.5: Own transport
A club that chooses to travel by its own means **receives no reimbursement**.

### Article 6.6: Transport failure
If federation transport fails or arrives late, **no team loses by forfeit**. The match is replayed on a Wednesday within three weeks.

## Chapter 7: Youth Beker van België

### Article 7.1: Age groups and wings
**U13 to U19**, played jointly by Voetbal Vlaanderen and FFA clubs. U13 plays 8v8. **Only first teams take part** (Article 4.12.6).

### Article 7.2: Qualification
Qualification is based on **the cohort's previous season** (for U13, the U12 season).

| Round | Who enters |
|---|---|
| Round 1 | **Provinciaal table winners**, plus the FFA's provincial-level qualifiers |
| Round 2 | Interprovinciaal teams that finished **in the top half** of their table, plus the FFA's interprovincial-level qualifiers |
| Round 3 | **All Elite 2 teams** |
| Round 4 | **All Elite 1 teams**; straight knockout to the final from here |

1. The FFA quotas are set every season so that **exactly 32 teams play round 4**.
2. **Byes only in rounds 1 to 3.** When an even number is impossible in those rounds, the remaining bye goes by draw to a team entering in that round.
3. **U13 for professional clubs:** a professional club's U13 enters at the round of the Elite level it plays (Article 4.2a).

### Article 7.3: Dates

| Stage | When |
|---|---|
| Rounds 1 to 6 | The six fixed cup Wednesdays |
| Semi-finals | The Saturday after the play-off window (Article 3.8) |
| Final | One Saturday later, at **Proximus Basecamp, Tubize** |

### Article 7.4: Home team
**The lower-level team plays at home.** When both teams play at the same level, the first team drawn plays at home.

### Article 7.5: Drawn matches

| Age groups | Rule |
|---|---|
| U13 to U15 | Straight to penalties |
| U16 to U19 | Two halves of extra time, then penalties |

### Article 7.6: Travel *(rewritten in v1.5)*

| Stage | Covered |
|---|---|
| Rounds 1 and 2 | Chapter 6: trips of **50 km or more** |

## Chapter 9: Launch (transition)

### Article 9.1: Structuring season
The **2025-26 season is played entirely under the old rules**. It is used to:
1. audit the Elite licences;
2. fix the geography (placements under Articles 2.3 and 2.4);
3. contract the federation travel;
4. set the calendar framework;
5. recruit and register the volunteer referees and referees in training (Chapter 10);
6. draw the 2026-27 tables.

### Article 9.2: Seeding for 2026-27
1. Every cohort **starts at the level it actually played in 2025-26**:

| Old level (2025-26) | New level (2026-27) |
|---|---|
| Elite | Elite 1 or Elite 2, subject to the licence |
| Interprovinciaal | IP |
| Provinciaal | P |
| Gewestelijk | G |

2. When a level has more teams than places, places go by the federation's 2025-26 ranking of those teams. **Teams that do not fit drop one level.**
3. When a level has fewer teams than places, it is **filled from the level below** by the same ranking.
4. The new U10 of 2026-27 is placed by **the entry rule** (Article 4.2).
5. The Elite 1 and Elite 2 split at launch follows where each academy **actually played in the real elite youth competitions in 2025-26**.
6. **Second and further teams** are seeded under **Article 4.12.1**, not by their 2025-26 level.
7. **Clubs holding an Elite licence for 2026-27** dissolve their second and further teams.
8. **An academy that played Elite in 2025-26 but did not meet the v1.4 sporting criterion** was treated as in v1.4 Article 5.7: Voetbal Vlaanderen clubs entered IP as an extra team; FFA clubs entered the equivalent FFA level.

### Article 9.3: Launch cup
In the 2026-27 Youth Beker van België, cup qualification uses **the 2026-27 seeding order** instead of a previous-season ranking.

### Article 9.4: Moving to v1.5 *(new)*
1. **2027-28 tables stand** as drawn under v1.4, including the licence decisions published on 30 April 2027 and the extra IP teams (KVC Westerlo, KV Kortrijk).
2. **Elite 2 is below target** in every age group (seven teams in 2027-28), so Article 4.8.4 applies: no relegation from Elite 2 until an age group reaches 16.
3. **The first IP-to-Elite 2 promotions** are earned at the end of 2027-28 and apply in 2028-29.
4. **Professional status and second teams** are applied under v1.5 from 2027-28: second teams of a non-professional club whose cohort plays Elite are dissolved at the end of 2027-28 (Article 4.12.7).
5. **Cup travel under Article 7.6** applies from the 2027-28 Youth Beker van België.
6. **The v1.4 rule of direct return to Elite 2 after a licence failure** (old Article 5.7, A30) is abolished. Westerlo and Kortrijk return through Article 4.8.

## Chapter 10: Refereeing

### Article 10.1: Gewestelijk
**A volunteer referee registered with Voetbal Vlaanderen**, provided by the home club. The away club can request a **neutral volunteer up to seven days before the match**.

### Article 10.2: Provinciaal
1. Volunteer referees, as in Article 10.1.
2. **Exception:** the **last two scheduled matchdays** of each table, and **every catch-up match played after the first of those two**, are refereed by **referees in training**.
3. When there are not enough referees in training for those matches, **official referees** cover them.

### Article 10.3: Interprovinciaal
All matches are refereed by **referees in training**.

### Article 10.4: Play-offs
**All play-off matches at every level** (arrondissement, provincial and relegation play-offs) are refereed by **referees in training**.

### Article 10.5: Youth Beker van België
1. Rounds 1 to 3: **referees in training**.
2. From round 4: referees from the **Elite system** (Article 10.6).

### Article 10.6: Elite 1 and Elite 2
Referees are **assigned by the KBVB referee department**, never provided by either club.

### Article 10.7: Definitions and minimum ages

| | Definition | Minimum age |
|---|---|---|
| **Referee in training** | Enrolled in the official Voetbal Vlaanderen referee course | **15** |
| **Volunteer referee** | Registered with Voetbal Vlaanderen after completing the online module | **16** |

### Article 10.8: No referee
1. If the assigned referee does not arrive, **any adult both coaches agree on** may referee.
2. If nobody can, it is **force majeure**: no forfeit, and the match is replayed on a Wednesday within three weeks (Article 3.4).

## Chapter 12: The Voetbal Vlaanderen app: profiles *(new in v1.6)*

### Article 12.1: What the app shows
The Voetbal Vlaanderen app shows **club profiles, team profiles and player profiles** for every competition in this rulebook. **Everyone can search them** (Article 12.5).

### Article 12.2: Player profiles
1. **From Provinciaal upwards** (Provinciaal, Interprovinciaal, Elite 2, Elite 1), every player on a match sheet has a **public player profile**. **Gewestelijk players have none**: on a Gewestelijk team page only the name appears (Article 12.4.3).
2. A player profile shows:
   1. **A profile picture**, uploaded by the club: the player's face in the club kit;
   2. **Current club** and **all previous clubs**, with the seasons;
   3. **Position(s), preferred foot, shirt number**;
   4. **Birth year** (not the full date of birth) and **nationality**;
   5. **Per season**: matches, starts, minutes, **goals, assists**, cards; **clean sheets** for goalkeepers and defenders (counted only with **at least 60 minutes** played);
   6. **Per season, the level, team and its final position** (for example: *2032-33 · FCV Dender EH U12 · IP · 1st*);
   7. **Honours** as badges: titles, promotions, cup wins, youth national-team caps.
3. A profile **never** shows height, weight, ratings, address, school or contact details.
4. Stats count from the season the player first plays at Provinciaal level or higher. Earlier Gewestelijk seasons appear only as club and level, without individual stats.
5. **International transfer.** When a player moves to a club in another association, **his profile stays public**:
   1. **Current club** shows the foreign club, its country and its league or age-group team (from the International Transfer Certificate);
   2. **All his seasons in Belgium stay**, with their stats, ending with **his last season here**;
   3. **No stats are kept for his seasons abroad**: they appear only as club and season;
   4. **If he returns** to a Belgian club, his stats continue from where they stopped.

### Article 12.3: Club profiles
A club profile shows:
1. **Name, matricule number, colours, crest**;
2. **Home ground** with address (for example: *Denderstraat, Ninove · De Kloppers*);
3. **Status**: professional or non-professional (Article 1.9), and the **Elite licence** if it holds one;
4. **Every team the club fields this season, by age group**, with its level (for example: *U10 · IP*, *U13 · Elite 1*); second teams marked **"B"** (Article 4.12);
5. **Club honours**: youth titles, promotions and cup wins, totalled.

### Article 12.4: Team profiles
1. A team profile shows:
   1. **Age group, level, table and current position**, the table one tap away;
   2. **Squad**: name, profile picture, position, shirt number, birth year. **From Provinciaal upwards** each name opens the player profile;
   3. **Staff**: head coach and assistants, with their coaching licence (UEFA C, B, A);
   4. **Fixtures and results**: league, Youth Beker and continental matches;
   5. **Form**: the last five results;
   6. **The cohort's level history**, one line per season (for example: *2030-31 · U10 · Gewestelijk · 1st, promoted*), under the cohort principle (Article 1.3);
   7. **The cohort's honours**;
   8. **Training ground and training days** (for example: *Denderstraat · Monday, Wednesday, Friday*). **Training days are public.**
2. **Team stats from Provinciaal upwards**: goals scored and conceded, clean sheets, **top scorer, top assister and most minutes played, by name**.
3. **Gewestelijk teams show team totals only**: goals scored and conceded, clean sheets, results. **No individual stats and no names in the stats.**
4. A team profile **never** shows a coach's or player's phone number, e-mail or home town.

### Article 12.5: Search and follow
1. **Search** by player name, club, team, and birth year. Gewestelijk players can be found by name only on their team's squad list.
2. **"Volgen"** (follow): a player, a team or a club. A followed player sends a notification for a **goal, an assist, or a clean sheet**; a followed team, for every result; a followed club, for every title, promotion and cup result.

### Article 12.6: Who enters the data
1. **The referee's match sheet** is the source for goals, cards, minutes and line-ups (Chapter 10).
2. **Assists** are entered by the **home club** within 48 hours and can be contested by the away club within a further 48 hours; the provincial committee decides.
3. **Profile pictures** are uploaded by the club, with the parents' consent for players under 18.
4. A club that enters false data loses its points for that match.

### Article 12.7: Match recording *(A46)*
1. **From Interprovinciaal upward, every match is recorded and put on the app**: every IP, Elite 2 and Elite 1 league match, and every Youth Beker match in which an IP or Elite team plays.
2. **Voetbal Vlaanderen supplies and pays for one fixed, automatic camera** at every ground where an IP or Elite team plays its home matches, so recording never depends on a club's budget or a parent with a phone. *(Closure: no club can skip recording for lack of means.)*
3. **The match page** on the app shows **the full match** and **automatic highlights** (goals, shots on target, saves) **within 24 hours**, linked to the line-ups, scorers and assists of Article 12.6.
4. **Full matches** can be watched by **every app user with a Voetbal Vlaanderen account**; **highlights** are public. *(Closure: the same audience as the player profiles of Article 12.2.)*
5. **Accounts are open to everyone** *(A47)*: anyone can create a free Voetbal Vlaanderen account, **without being a player, parent, coach or club member**. An account gives no club role and no access to a club's internal data.
6. **If the camera fails**, the home club must upload its own recording within 48 hours; **three matches without a recording in one season cost the home club a fine** set by the provincial committee. *(Closure: no "the camera was broken" every week.)*
7. **Clubs may not withhold, cut or delay a recording**; disputed incidents go to the referee's report, not the video. *(Closure: the video is not used to change results.)*
