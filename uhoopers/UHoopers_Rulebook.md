# UHoopers Rulebook
### *Unlock the Hooper within* · @uhoopers

*UHoopers is the user's own youth basketball development programme in Los Angeles, built on the Uplayer model (`uplayer/Uplayer_Rulebook.md`). **It exists only in the US Basketball universe campaign it was created for** (every campaign is a separate universe). It is **not a club**: it never registers players or fields teams in the pyramid (Rulebook Art. 22B).*

## 2. Groups

| Group | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Development** | **Everyone enrolled** trains in the Development group of his **band**. **$1,500 a year**, or **free when invited by UHoopers**. Paying or invited makes no difference: same band, sessions and selection |
| **Competitive** | **Not a separate group.** Players are selected from the Development sessions for **domestic friendlies**: **7–9 per age group per season**. UHoopers pays all travel and housing for away matches. The friendlies never count in any competition (Art. 7.5, Art. 22B) |
| **Pro and Pro+** | **Not yet.** UHoopers has no reputation; the user may add them later |

## 3. Who can join

**Anyone.** A player registered at a **tier 1–3 club** needs his **club's written permission** (Art. 22B). Tier-4 players and players without a club need nothing.

## 4. Selection for Competitive

> **60% scrim performance + 40% training**, where training counts **performance first, effort second, attitude third** (as Uplayer)

## 5. Staff

**Three perfect coaches per band (15 in all).** A perfect coach has **perfect tactical intelligence, perfect man management, perfect training, perfect scouting and perfect player vision**. **There are no perfect players.**

In games, a perfect coach makes every change the moment it's needed; after every loss the model checks whether a perfect coach could have prevented it.

## 6. Training

The four pillars, ranked by frequency (**tactical most, mental least**): **tactical** (decisions, reads, sets), **technical** (ball-handling, shooting, passing, footwork), **physical** (strength, speed, power, biomechanics), **mental** (confidence, focus). **Session mix (out of 10): 4 tactical, 3 technical, 2 physical, 1 mental. Every session ends with a scrimmage.** On a Sunday: a group session, an individual session for one player while the rest do the day's pillar work (players rotate), then a scrimmage or a Competitive game.

## 8. Partner clubs

UHoopers' coaches look for **three established Youth SD1 or AAND clubs in the Los Angeles area**:

| Partner | What they get |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | **UHoopers coaches for free.** They co-coach the club's youth teams (its senior team if it has no youth) |
| **Secondary** | **UHoopers coaches for a fixed monthly fee**, all year, to co-coach its youth; **its seniors are trained for free** |
| **Back-up** | **Everything the secondary gets**, at a **fair market price** |

## 9. The contract service

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | One UHoopers coach searches for a professional contract for the player, **for 3 months** |
| **Price** | **$20** the first time; **each new order costs 50% more** than the last for that player ($20, $30, $45, …) |
| **The 5%** | **Only from 15½**: if a contract comes out of the search, **5% of that contract's full value** goes to UHoopers, paid as the money comes in. **Only that contract**: not later ones |
| **Which contract** | The one signed **during the 3 months**, or with a club the coach introduced if signed **within 6 months after** (closure: no waiting until day 91) |
| **Under 15½** | The search runs **without the 5%**. It can only start when Art. 35 allows an approach (from 13½ for a player on course for 6'3"), and a contract is only possible from 14 (6'3", measured twice) or 16 |
| **Minors** | A parent co-signs; the 5% is taken before the rest goes into the player's trust (Art. 34) |
| **Agents** | Not while the player has an agent searching for him at the same time |
