# Uplayer Rulebook

*Uplayer is the user's own youth football programme in the Ninove/Dender area. **It exists only in football sim universes set in 2024 or later.** In RBVA universes it runs **Development only** (no Competitive, Pro or Pro+).*

---

## 1. Identity

| | |
|---|---|
| **Founders and funding** | **Co-founded by KVK Ninove**, also funded by **KFC Voorde-Appelterre** |
| **KVK Ninove link** | **Every session of every KVK Ninove age group is co-coached by at least one Uplayer coach.** Uplayer coaches KVK's senior team every session and every age group three times a month. **In these universes Voetbal Vlaanderen places all KVK Ninove age groups (U6–U9 included) in Interprovinciaal.** KVK's youth trains Monday, Wednesday and Friday (real schedule) |
| **Day** | **Sundays** |
| **Consent** | Joining means agreeing that minors' faces are shown on social media |

## 2. Groups

| Group | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Development** | **Everyone enrolled** plays in the Development group of his band. **€1,200 a year**, or **free when invited by Uplayer**. Paying or invited makes no difference: same band, sessions and selection |
| **Competitive** | **Not a separate group.** Players are selected from the Development sessions for elite matches. **7–9 matches per age group per season** (12 for Pro), **always against international opponents**, never domestic. Uplayer pays all travel and housing for away Competitive matches. Exists for every age group |
| **Pro** (from U15) | A separate group, Competitive by nature: **12 Pro players + 6 Development-selected players per match**. Reviews in **November, January, March and May**; a place isn't guaranteed and players can move up or down. **At U18 Pro:** senior opposition (second divisions); Uplayer actively seeks professional trials and contracts for non-professional players and prepares them for Pro+ |
| **Pro+** (from U19) | **For domestic professionals** (players under contract at Belgian clubs) who were in Pro. A Pro+ player can choose it without a Pro coach's recommendation. **Entry trial:** three sessions + one match as a substitute, then accepted or rejected (no monitoring option; he can try again later) |
| **Release** | A player who has gone professional at a club **outside Belgium** and can't make training is released |

## 3. Selection for Competitive

> **60% scrim performance + 40% training**, where training counts **performance first, effort second, attitude third**

## 4. Staff and licences

| | |
|---|---|
| **Non-Pro bands** | 73% UEFA B, 27% UEFA A |
| **Pro bands** | 54% UEFA Pro, 46% UEFA A |
| **Pro staff** | One individual coach per two players; per position one psychologist, two strength coaches, two physios and two video-analysis coaches |

## 5. Training

### 5.1 The four pillars

Ranked by training frequency, **tactical most, mental least**:

| Pillar | Examples (every attribute of the pillar is trained) |
|---|---|
| **Tactical** | Decisions, tactics |
| **Technical** | First touch, passing, dribbling |
| **Physical** | Strength, speed, power, biomechanics |
| **Mental** | Self-esteem, confidence |

**Session mix (out of 10):** 4 tactical, 3 technical, 2 physical, 1 mental. **Every session ends with a scrim.**

### 5.2 A typical Sunday

1. **General group session**
2. **Individual session for one boy**, while the rest do the day's pillar group session (a tactical day → tactical group work). **The boys rotate**, one per session
3. **Match**

## 6. Philosophy

| Format | System |
|---|---|
| **5v5** | 1-2-1 / 2-1-1 |
| **8v8** | 2-3-2 in attack, 3-2-2 high press, tiki-taka |
| **11v11** | 4-3-3 in attack (one CAM, two CMs), 4-4-2 high press |
| **Always** | **Playing out from the back is crucial** |

## 8. In the development model

> **Uplayer bonus = +0.05 + 0.03 × (his individual Sundays ÷ Sundays attended)**

A boy who had an individual session every Sunday would get the full +0.08. In a rotation of 16 boys it is about +0.052. See `guides/03_Models.md`.
